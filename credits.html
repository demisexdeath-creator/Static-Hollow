    Built into the mod

    • Orbital Railgun by Mishkis, from Rayness's Renewed port (the NeoForge port it was taken from), both under the MIT licence (see LICENSE-orbital-railgun.txt in the mod). The Ray Gun and the Omni are built on it: its gun model, its texture, its sounds and its strike.
    • Static Hollow's own gun pack for Timeless and Classics Guns: Zero (the Mended SMG, Shotgun and Marksman Rifle), built into the Fabric mod.

    Ideas the README credits

    These gave the mod an idea. In each case the README says nothing of theirs is used.

    • Tectonic (MIT): lava glowing at the very bottom of the deep oceans. The deep is the game's own terrain, stretched.
    • Olaf's Relatively Placed Blocks: small blocks sharing one space.
    • Tough As Nails: the cold and the thirst.
    • Subnautica (1, 2 and Below Zero), Alex's Caves and Thalassophobia: the Dead Water's seas. Every creature, model, texture and sound there is Static Hollow's own.

    Tools

    • Blockbench for the creatures' models. The project files come separately as StaticHollow-Blockbench.zip.
    • Real-ESRGAN, an AI upscaler, for the optional HD packs (and since 1.61.0 the chapter gear in them).

    This website

    • Every story detail, item description, note and line of dialogue comes from the mod's own files (its README, language file, data and source), version 1.63.3.
    • The screenshots are the mod's own CurseForge screenshots, and the soundtrack cover and the Dead Channel logo are the mod's. The home page's scene is rebuilt from the mod's own main-menu layers, and the item and advancement icons are the mod's own textures. The cover art is the mod's CurseForge image.
    • Fonts, all under the SIL Open Font License and included with the site: Big Shoulders Display (Patric King), Spectral (Production Type) and Courier Prime (Alan Dague-Greene).
