    Photosensitivity warning

    Tune-In Town's night shift has flashing lights, flickering screens, loud noises and jumpscares. The game warns you before it starts, and once you say yes it keeps you there until you've survived five nights. If flashing images affect you, don't take the job. See also the warning on the install page.

    What it is

    Tune-In Town's show went out live on Channel 6 every Saturday from its own stage, its cast of animatronics running off reel-to-reel showtapes, till the Saturday the tape played HIM (the First Signal). It shut. Now he's opening it again. It's a secret Easter egg: a night shift in a pizza place, with twelve animatronics of its own coming for your office. The cast are on Tune-In Town's cast.

    The HELP WANTED flyer and the Old Set

    Since 1.65.3 the flyer is on the television. From 1.65.3 to 1.77.2 it could come any time after your first day; since 1.77.3 it waits for the story: none of the ways below brings it until the Last Broadcast has signed off. Anything already found stays found, and /statichollow eggs early opens it in a world now (/statichollow eggs story goes back to waiting).

    • An Old Set that's on, one you've crafted and put down anywhere in your world, once in a long while stops showing static and shows the flyer, held up close to the camera: "The set's stopped showing static. A flyer, held up close to the camera - yellowed, creased: HELP WANTED. TUNE-IN TOWN PIZZA & PLAYHOUSE. Then it clicks off - and the flyer's in your hand. Read it: the address."
    • One dark night, out in the open, you come on a set someone's left switched on in the grass a little way in front of you, showing it. Look at it (or use it) and the set clicks off and the flyer's in your hand.
    • On Chapter Ten's third day, while you're home, three knocks come at your door: someone's left a set on your step, facing in, on, showing static till the door's opened. Then it's the flyer. Since 1.72.2 it comes even if you've already seen the flyer or found the old place (a world past its third day gets it the next time you're home by day). Since 1.77.3 it, too, waits for the story's end.

    The HELP WANTED Flyer reads "Tune-In Town Pizza & Playhouse: Night Security, 12AM-6AM. Apply within. (It was on the television.)" Use it to read the address, which way and how far: "TUNE-IN TOWN PIZZA & PLAYHOUSE - so many blocks, that way". When the flyer comes, the old place is there, a little way off: "Somewhere close, the old place is there now - TUNE-IN TOWN. Read the flyer for the way." If there's nowhere for it yet (out at sea, up in the mountains), "The address is nowhere near here yet - try somewhere open and dry, out of the water and off the mountains." It comes the moment you're somewhere open and dry.

    The old place

    What's left of Tune-In Town in your world: shut years ago, a building about twenty-three blocks wide on a checker-tiled floor, the windows boarded, the dining room's tables and chairs where they were left, the little stage with its curtain in rags, the arcade dead, the sign over the door. Read the flyer when you get there: "This is the place. STAFF ONLY - at the back, by the stage." At the back, by the stage, is a steel door that isn't rotten at all: STAFF ONLY. Through it is Studio B.

    Since 1.65.3 it's shut, not fallen: its roof and walls are whole, its floor level with the ground round it, nothing of the world comes through inside (no dirt, moss, gravel, rubble or cobwebs), and the trees, hillside or pond where it stands are cleared right up past its roof. Nothing of it breaks, by hand or by any blast: "It won't break. Nothing of this place will." If the door won't open: "STAFF ONLY. It won't open yet - something's moving about behind it. Try again in a moment."

    Studio B

    Through the STAFF ONLY door is the place as it was on television, all of it, lit and empty: "TUNE-IN TOWN - Studio B. The real one." Going through earns Now Hiring. You come in at the lobby, at the front (the south). Since 1.71.1 it's furnished as a real restaurant, and since 1.65.3 it's darker: the dining hall a gloom under its high roof, the rooms off it dim, the halls near black. The front doors bring you back out into the old place.

    You're staff there, not a builder: you can't break or place anything (in creative you can, but not while a shift's on: "You're on the clock - no building, no breaking."). Whatever was built or broken is put right when the next night starts, and anything dropped is cleared.

    After hours

    Off the clock the cast stand at their places (on the stage, in the kitchen, behind the curtain, in the box, in the ball pit) switched on, their heads following you round the room. Come too near, make a noise or hit one, and it's awake and hunting you; lose it and it goes back to its place; kill it and it's off a few minutes, then back, rebooted. Now and then, after hours, the band on the stage go into each other and come for you as the Mended Band: "On the stage, the band have gone into each other. There's only one of them now - and it can see you." All of it is on Tune-In Town's cast.

    The secret: kept till five nights

    Since 1.65.2 the night shift is only played one way. Walk into the security office the first time and a WARNING comes up:

    • GO BACK (or Esc): "You back out of the office, to the lobby." You can look round Studio B as much as you like; the time clock won't take your card till you've said yes.
    • CONTINUE: "You've taken the job. Nobody goes home till they've worked 5 nights. Punch the time clock on the office wall."
    • Kept: the front doors won't let you out ("Not yet. You're staff now - so many more nights before you can go."), you wake up in the lobby if you die, and anything that puts you somewhere else puts you straight back.
    • Free: 6 AM on the fifth night. "FIVE NIGHTS. You did it - the doors are open, you're free to go. (The night shift's yours whenever you want it.)" After that it's yours to play whenever you like, with no warning and no keeping. Anyone who'd already made it through five nights is free already.

    The night shift

    Punch the time clock in the security office: "CLOCKED IN - Night 1. It's 12 AM. Your shift ends at 6." (Clocked In.) The cast come for the office, each its own way, and the office TV plays Hal's orientation tape for the night:

    The nights and the Custom Night

    • Nights one to five get harder: since 1.71.1 the first is three of them, gently, the second five, and the rest ramp up to the sixth. None of them stirs for the first moments of a night, longest on the first two. Beating the fifth is beating the secret.
    • The sixth night is the Encore's, there for the brave once you've lasted five (Overtime: "Make it through the sixth night - and the Encore").
    • Picking a night: sneak and use the time clock to pick which unlocked night your next shift is ("Next shift: Night 3 (sneak + use the clock to change - Night 4 is the newest)"); after the newest it goes back round to Night 1. After 6 AM it's the newest again.
    • The Custom Night, once the sixth is beaten: sneak and use the clock till it says CUSTOM NIGHT, then use it. Set how hard each of the twelve comes, from 0 (not at all) to 20 (shift-click goes by 5; ALL 0, ALL 20), and START. It's paid by how hard it was. Beating it with every one on 20 earns Twenty Twenty.

    On a server

    Since 1.66.6 the night shift is everyone's. The time clock won't punch till everyone on the server is in the security office and has taken the job ("Everyone clocks in together - waiting on them to come to the security office."), and those it's waiting on are told they're wanted. Then you're all on the clock together, on the night the furthest behind of you is up to: one power, one set of doors and lights, one music box, and each of you your own monitor (any of you can wind the box or run a REBOOT).

    • Whoever gets in gets the one of you nearest it: they're out, thrown out to the lobby, and the rest keep going. It's only lost if it gets all of you, or the power goes.
    • Make it to 6 AM and the night is everyone's who clocked in, caught or not: all paid, all on to the next night together.
    • Walk off the job and you're off the shift (if you were the last, they come after you).
    • Picking a night goes as far as the whole server has unlocked; the Custom Night once everyone's beaten the sixth.

    Commands

    • /statichollow locate tune_in_town and /statichollow locate flyer_tv (since 1.66.7): where the old place is, and the Old Set that last showed the flyer. Click the answer to go there. If the old place isn't anywhere yet, asking from the Overworld sets it down near you; if no set's showing the flyer, one's left on near you.
    • /statichollow goto tune_in_town (or flyer_tv): takes you there, onto the ground (in front of the Old Set, looking at it).
    • /locate structure statichollow:tune_in_town and statichollow:flyer_tv find them too.
    • /statichollow tunein release <players> (for server operators): lets someone out without beating it.
    • /statichollow tunein skipnights [players] (with cheats on, since 1.72.1): all six nights behind you and straight to the Custom Night, with what the nights would have given you: "All six nights, behind you. The Custom Night's open - pick it on the clock."

    Advancements here

    • Now Hiring: Go through the STAFF ONLY door into Studio B
    • Clocked In: Start a night shift at Tune-In Town
    • 6 AM: Make it through a night shift
    • You're Fired: Get jumpscared on the night shift
    • Have You Tried Turning It Off?: On the night shift, reboot the one HIS static's in
    • Overtime: Make it through the sixth night - and the Encore
    • Disbanded: Take apart the Mended Band
    • Twenty Twenty: Beat a Custom Night with every one of them on 20
    • The Owner: Win all three gold stars on Tune-In Town's time clock
    • Encore for One: Have the band play the whole show, just for you
