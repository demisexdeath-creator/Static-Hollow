    What it is

    A still black sea as far as you can see, a realm made of the First Signal's static: it crawls in the walls of the world, far off all round. There's no sky, only black, and a dark fog. On the sea stands his castle, cut out of his static: black stone with it crawling in it, its walls set with dead sets, its towers and its great mast blinking red. A causeway runs out to it from the platform where he comes in. Under the water in front of it, lit, are the windows into every realm he's touched, each with a cable running back to the castle's foot. Ash comes down the whole time, and his static drifts along the ground.

    You first see it in the Broadcast. Once the Harbinger has gone, the next time you're home it cuts in on everyone in the Overworld: the First Signal tears his way out of the Hub, comes out of his tear on this platform and walks the causeway; the Harbinger comes up out of the water to him; in his war hall he lays his hand on his map, and his gate opens and his creations pour out, down into the windows, into yours. The score of the kingdom's part of it is On Air. The whole scene is on Chapter Ten.

    Since 1.68.0 everything of his is there whenever anyone's in it (the windows lit, the static round it all, his map); before, some of it was only there during the Broadcast.

    Getting in, and out

    His sites come through into your world after the Broadcast: a Transmitter rising out of the ground, a Burrow eaten out of the rock in the caves, a Set going up at night out in the open. Each has a Broadcast Core. Break it and the site goes off the air, and since 1.64.2 "Where it stood, his signal's torn open. It won't stay that way long." The tear is like the one he comes through, colour bars rolling in it and its edges crackling, and it stays open for a minute. Step into it and you're on his platform at the end of the causeway, his castle across the black water: "His kingdom. Behind you, the way back." That earns His Guest.

    • Out: behind you on the platform is a redder tear, the way back, to just where you were.
    • Staying: while you're his guest his realm doesn't throw you out.
    • The first time after the Broadcast, he finds you there: the Refusal. He comes through his own static to the platform, takes you by the throat ("GET. OUT.") and throws you back out through your tear, hard, and it shuts behind you: "Thrown out of his kingdom." Anyone in there with you goes too, and nobody's hurt. For the rest of that day his static stands across the causeway between his platform and his castle; come back through a tear and there's no getting past it (it throws you back).
    • In part four of the Last Broadcast, anyone who comes to his kingdom is carried straight on through to his arena in the End.

    The sites, his creations and the rest of the chapter are on Chapter Ten and his creations and servants.

    The platform and the causeway

    You come in at the south end, on his platform: a deck of his bricks over the water, seventeen blocks wide, a low wall round its edge and two lamp posts, one either side of where his tear comes. From it the causeway runs north to his island, seven blocks wide, with low walls along it, lamp posts every twelve blocks, and piers going down into the sea. The island is about seventy blocks across and ninety long, its stone walls going down to the floor, its top a floor of bricks, a low wall round its edge with a lamp post at each corner.

    Left of the causeway, in front of the castle and under his balcony's view, is where the Harbinger comes up out of the water in the Broadcast. From the third day (two days after the Refusal) it's out in the water round the castle: up out of the sea, over, and down again, now here, now there, well out from the castle and never over the causeway.

    His castle

    The keep fills most of the island: fifty-three blocks across, its walls thirty-six blocks high, of Signal Bricks with his static in them and Dead Screens let into them in rows, looking out, battlements along the top. Four towers stand at its corners, nine blocks across, going up past the keep, each with a spire of bars and red lamps up it. In the front wall is the gate, an arch nine blocks wide and twelve high with its portcullis (it's let down and drawn up as things happen), and over it the balcony, with a doorway out onto it from the floor inside, where he comes out. On the keep's roof stands the great mast, a lattice going up to ninety-two blocks over the sea, with a red lamp every so often and one at its top. A bell tolls up it.

    The courtyard

    Between the end of the causeway and the gate, on the island in front of the keep, is the courtyard. The Faithful keep two of their circles there, one either side of the way to the gate, and walk round it in slow procession (see below).

    The war hall

    The keep's ground floor, behind a wall across the end of the gate's passage: pillars down both sides, a ceiling eighteen blocks up, and down the middle his war table, a slab of black stone on brick legs, eighteen blocks long. His chair is at its head, high-backed. Red lamps hang low over the table in two rows, there's a lamp on each pillar's faces toward the table, and dead sets are set round the walls: a hall lit red by his lamps, the rest of it dark. In the Broadcast his three servants stand at the table.

    His map of your world

    In the Broadcast his map lights up under his hand. From the day after the Refusal it isn't the one it was: it's your world, worked out from your world's own ground (nothing's made up). Your house is in the middle, ringed in red, with two thousand blocks either side of it and more along the length of the table: the sea and the lakes, the rivers, the shores, the woods, the deserts, the snow, the heights in contour, and his red grid over it every five hundred blocks. Lit on it, as they move: any of you out in your world, his creations, and his sites.

    The windows under the sea

    Under the black water in front of the castle, six either side of the causeway, lie twelve windows, each twelve blocks across and lit, each looking into a realm he's touched (the names below are what each shows, not names the game gives them):

    West of the causewayEast of the causeway
    The HollowThe Bloom
    The glasshouseThe Deep
    The flowerThe Dead Water
    The cityThe Hub
    The NullYour world: the one his creations go into
    The StallsThe static: the one still dark

    From every window a cable runs to the island's foot. Since 1.67.0, when the sea went down two hundred blocks, each window lies on a pillar of his stone coming up out of the deep (its top a slab twelve blocks under the surface, its shaft narrowing down into the dark and flaring out at its foot), and the cables run between them at that depth, held up on posts every ten blocks. You can still see them, lit, under the water.

    A kingdom made before 1.67.0 is made again, with his castle and his Faithful set down in it again. The old one is kept as a folder, the_kingdom-before-1.67, in your world's dimensions/statichollow.

    The Faithful at their rites

    Since 1.64.2 his congregation is in his castle: the Faithful, tall, in hooded robes of a red so dark it's nearly black, a little red light hung at the breast, and under the hood a black hole where a face goes. They keep his rites:

    • in circles of red and black candles round a lamp set in the floor, kneeling, rocking, chanting; praising on their knees, arms up, swaying; bowed flat, arms out along the floor, bowing again and again: two circles in the courtyard, four in the war hall's aisles behind the pillars;
    • kneeling by his war table, facing it: eight, four a side, back from it behind his servants;
    • in slow procession, hands clasped, hoods bowed: three walking the causeway from his platform to the gate and back, three round the courtyard.

    They don't stop for you, but their hoods turn after you wherever you go. Come right up to one and the whole circle stops, every hood on you, till you go. Hurt one and every one of them for twenty-four blocks turns on you, the chant breaking into a scream. Get far enough away and they go back to their places and kneel again. Killed, the robe falls empty, and those killed stay dead. Now and then (one in five, since 1.69.0) one drops the dagger he made them: Faithful's Dagger. More on them in his creations and servants.

    Nothing's quiet there

    Its sound is his static, with the chant far off in it, and the voices through the windows. And now and then, from somewhere:

    • whispering, just behind you (nobody there);
    • footsteps behind you, coming up, and stopping;
    • chanting, far off across the water;
    • a bell tolling up his mast;
    • iron groaning: his gate on its hinges;
    • a music box somewhere, winding down in the middle of its tune;
    • someone crying, inside, through the walls.

    His arena

    In the Coronation (the Last Broadcast, part two) his arena comes up out of the sea off his island's east side. A round floor about forty blocks across, a wall round it, seven tiers of seats for his Faithful round that, his own box high on its north side with a throne in it, an outer wall going down into the sea, and lamps round its top. Its gate is on its west side, with a bridge from it to his island. The Watcher stands inside by the gate. Step down onto its floor and he comes down off his box at you; stay off it long enough and he's back in his box, whole. It stays there whenever you come back, until part four.

    In the Tearing (part four) the whole of it is torn up out of his sea into the sky, and you with it.

    His arena in the End

    It comes down in the End: hanging out over the void north of the End's island, a ragged cone of the rock it was torn out of under it, its bridge snapped off short, pieces of it hanging loose in the air round it. While all this is going on the End lets you in to it; the rest of the time the End still sends you home. Fall off its edge into the void and his static catches you and puts you back on the floor (it hurts). The fights in it are on the Last Broadcast.

    SENTINEL's wreck

    In the Fall (part three) the sky over his castle rips open right across the north and SENTINEL comes through it, the whole station. He tears its right side off. That half comes down burning into his sea far out and lies there with its broken end up out of the water; one module of it comes down near, east of his arena.

    That module stays, real blocks: lying tilted in the sea, its hatch end up out of the water and its far end torn open, scorched, the parasites' resin in it; its windows, its deck, its lamps (some out); bits of its solar wing and a girder round it; fires on its back. Its crew's chest is still in it, with SENTINEL - Crew Log among what's in there. What's left of SENTINEL hangs dark in the rip over it all.

    Since 1.70.0 his kingdom's wall of static stands further out, past where the wreck came down.

    What's there after each part

    The kingdom keeps what each part of the story leaves in it:

    AfterWhat you find
    The BroadcastHis castle, the windows, the Faithful at their rites (from 1.64.2); him at his war table
    The Refusal (part one)That day, his static across the causeway. From the next day his static's gone, he isn't at his table, and his map is of your world; the Faithful at their rites as they were
    The third dayThe Harbinger in the water round the castle. Walk up the causeway (or wait) and the Coronation plays
    The Call (part two)His arena off the island's east side. Him on his roof at his mast, the Watcher knelt in the arena with the Omni up at him, the tear in the sky over it all
    The Forge (part three)Him in his box, mended, enchanted, alight with SENTINEL's power; what's left of SENTINEL dark in the rip; the Watcher knelt by the gate with the Omni; the wreck in the sea
    The Rebirth (part four)His arena gone from his sea; only SENTINEL's wreck and what's left of SENTINEL, dark in the rip. And he's gone

    Its blocks

    • Signal Stone: his static, cut into stone (the sea's floor, the island, the windows' pillars)
    • Signal Bricks, with their wall, slab and stairs
    • Dead Screen: the dead sets in his walls
    • Signal Lamp: his red lamps
