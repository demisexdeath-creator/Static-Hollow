    The First Signal Speaks

    take them off the air.

    On this page
    1. Chapter seven
    2. Chapter ten
    3. The Last Broadcast

    The First Signal hardly speaks, and never with a name on his words like the Watcher's. In a cutscene they come up low in the picture, typed out a letter at a time as if over a bad line, white with red and blue coming apart round them; when he shouts, they fill the middle of the screen, shaking. In a fight they come in chat, in dark red, bold when he shouts. Here is everything he says, from Chapter Seven on, in story order. (In Chapters Seven and Eight the Watcher calls him "it"; from Chapter Ten on he says "he".)

    Lines with no name on them are his, as they are in the game. ??? is the Watcher, as he's named in game, and SENTINEL's crew are shown as their radio shows them. What anyone else says is in the grey notes between. Lines in blue are on-screen text from the same scenes.

    Chapter seven

    The Hub: his speech is cut short ("what do you-"), and the sky over the gallery tears open. Low, huge, more than one voice at once

    ENOUGH.

    He tears the rift wide and comes through. The Watcher shoves him away, and they jump

    ???jump.

    That's the only word he says in Chapter Seven. After it, past the wall the Watcher shut in the Hub, he's only ever heard, never in words ("Something past the wall"). He says nothing in Chapters Eight and Nine: "it can't hear us here. not through all this water." The voice that speaks when the Harbinger rises in Chapter Nine is the two hearts', not his.

    Chapter ten

    The Broadcast

    The wall: in the Hub, both hands flat on the static. He stops; his head tilts; then a scream of many voices through a dead channel. He tears his own way out, to his kingdom, and walks the causeway. The Harbinger comes up out of the water, hung on its strings

    look at you.

    you went down an animal.

    you came up mine.

    stronger. so much stronger.

    It lowers its skull to his hand. In his war hall, his servants round his table, his hand on the map of your world

    it's time.

    find them. both of them.

    take them off the air.

    His gate opens, and his creations pour out into the windows. At your house:

    ???do you feel that?

    ???it's everywhere. all over the world, all at once.

    ???that much signal - all of it his.

    ???get ready.

    CHAPTER TEN / STAY TUNED / they're coming

    His creations have voices of their own (the Announcer's "STAY TUNED." among them). They're on his creations and servants.

    The Refusal
