    The Watcher speaks in chat, in lower case, a line at a time, and only at the moments that matter. Here is what it says, in story order. Lines in blue are on-screen text from the same scenes.

    His lines from 1.47.2 to 1.63.3 (after signing off, the Hive and the Deep, the Door at the End of the World, the Hub and the Dead Water) are here too, along with a few older ones this page used to be missing: chapter two's first kills, the Mended, and the glasshouse.

    What he wrote on the chart

    Point at a water on the Watcher's Chart and you get his note on it.

    The Grey Shelf

    Grey water over grey silt. The rig stands in it. Nothing here is in a hurry - mostly.

    The Haemal Reach

    The water's thick with blood. Clotfish come in swarms; the Siphons wait for them to pass.

    The Wireweed

    Weed like wire, and it sings. The Choir Eels nest in it. Don't touch the bright ones.

    The Lantern Fields

    Lights in the dark as far as you can see. Most of them are bulbs. Some of them aren't.

    The Ossuary Flats

    A plain of bone. Whatever died here, died big. The Stiltfins pick it clean.

    The Brine Sink

    The brine pools eat what falls in. The water's sour, and the Puffbloats like it.

    The Molar Reef

    Teeth, grown into a reef. The Grinners live in it. They smile at you.

    The Drowned Mycelia

    Mushrooms the size of towers, drowned. Their spores burst if you brush them.

    The Rime Shelf

    Ice under the sea. Things are frozen in it. Some of them wake up.

    The Ember Vents

    Vents roaring up from below. Warm. The Mantlecrabs crowd round them - mind your power.

    The Carrier Wave

    The static's loudest here. The water hums. Your instruments won't like it.

    The Black Below

    THE DEAD ZONE. The floor drops away and doesn't stop. The leviathans live down there. Don't.
