    The Turning

    Once ORPHEUS has all three of her modules in her bay (the pressure plating, the depth module and the UV field module) it plays, for everyone in the Dead Water: you're taken off whatever you're in (she's put back in her clamps), out of any screen, and up onto the rig's helipad. A full cutscene with its own score, The Turning.

    The Undertow comes up out of the sea by the rig and roars. Then it goes. To the Drowned Mycelia, down through the roof of the Grove's hollow, where the Grove Heart flinches:

    Its jaws close on it. Then to the Haemal Reach, and through the Drowned Heart. With both of them in it, it turns: the Grove's fungus up its left side, pale, gilled, caps growing out of it; the Reach's flesh up its right, raw and veined, its light gone red. It comes back mad, up out of the sea hundreds of blocks and down, again and again round the rig (since 1.63.25 its first time up is out past a hundred blocks off the rig), and the last time it comes down by the rig, and lies there.

    Down under where he went into the sea, the Watcher wakes. His bubble breaks, and he comes up out of the sea and onto the helipad beside you. You point at it.

    Then the card: CHAPTER NINE - THE HARBINGER - three days. Watching it all earns The Turning ("Watch the Undertow eat both hearts - and come down by the rig"). The whole chapter is told on Chapter Nine.

    The three days

    "Chapter Nine: The Harbinger. It lies in the sea by the rig, both infections all over it - he says it's sleeping. Three days."

    The husk lies out in the sea north-east of the rig (on your left, looking out to sea from the helipad), its jaws a hundred blocks off the rig and its whole body there. Its eyes are shut. Both infections are all over it, spores steaming off one side and blood off the other, and its heart can be heard from the rig, beating faster as the days go. There's no fight: it just lies there. While it does, nothing comes up out of the Black Below, the Grove's Heart doesn't grow back and the Drowned Heart doesn't beat.

    The Watcher keeps watch at the helipad's rail. Come up to him and he has something to say, a day at a time:

    The three days are 72,000 ticks. The Dead Water's sky never moves and nobody sleeps there, so since 1.63.24 they count by the overworld's clock too: /time add, or nights slept through at home, bring it on.

    What came out of it

    Three days on, the next time you're in the Dead Water, what's in it comes out. Its bones come out through its mouth: a skull, a spine and its ribs, strung all through with the Grove's strings, which run out of the heart in its ribs, where the Grove's heart and the Reach's are going into one. Hung on its strings, it rises over the sea, retches, and brings up a static heart at your feet (one for each of you). The heart speaks to you both, two voices whispering as one:

    It tears a way out of the Dead Water, half the Grove's and half the Reach's, and goes through it. The way shuts, and the husk goes down into the sea.

    Seeing it earns This Gift Will Not Be Wasted ("See what came out of it after three days"). After it, nothing comes up out of the Black Below any more.

    The static heart

    "It's gone through. The static heart it brought up is on the helipad - put it in the Remote (craft them together)." The Static Heart (Mutated) crafted with the Broken Remote (or the mended one) makes the Remote (Mutated): it works again, and the Undertow's roar comes out of your hand. See chapter gear.

    His

    Where it went is his kingdom. In Chapter Ten's Broadcast, as the First Signal walks his causeway, the water heaves and it comes up out of it, hung on its strings, and lowers its skull to his hand:

    Since 1.69.0, two days after the Refusal, it's in the black water round his castle: up out of the sea, over, and down again, now here, now there (well out from his castle, never over his causeway), swimming on under the water between. At the Coronation it comes up out of the sea behind him, and he draws his blade out of it: huge, black, curved, alive, the Undertow's own teeth along its edge and an eye in it.

    The hive

    When SENTINEL's torn-off module comes down in his sea, the parasites it brought up out of Blackwater take its crew, and the Chorus's last voice goes into the last of them. The Harbinger comes up out of the sea beside the wreck and swallows him whole, and the hive gets into it: its bones go violet. A door of the hive's chitin opens on the air out past the wreck, and it goes out through it and is gone.

    The Rebirth

    Straight after the Second Rite (1.71.0): far out in the dark, the Harbinger's bones. Its skin grows back over them, from its skull to its tail (since 1.72.0), all three of them in it, grown over each other: the Grove's, the Reach's, the hive's. Its eye opens.

    A door of the hive's opens, and it goes through; out of another, far off; and into another. THE LAST BROADCAST - THE SECOND RITE - to be continued.
