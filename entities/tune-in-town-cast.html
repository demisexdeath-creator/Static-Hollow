    Case file: the cast of tune-in town

    Tune-In Town's cast

    Hello? Is someone there? I just want to talk.

    On this page
    1. After hours
    2. On the clock
    3. The twelve
    4. HIS static
    5. The Mended Band
    6. Golden Tony

    Tune-In Town's show went out live on Channel 6 every Saturday from its own stage, its cast of animatronics running off reel-to-reel showtapes, till the Saturday the tape played HIM. It shut. Now he's opening it again. These are the twelve in Studio B, each with its own model, walk, tricks, jumpscare and death.

    Tune-In Town is a secret Easter egg, and its night shift has flashing lights, flickering screens, loud noises and jumpscares. The game warns you before you're hired.

    Studio B, through the STAFF ONLY doorHostile after hours
    How many
    Twelve: the band, the Raceway's hound, the host, the jester, the endoskeleton, the chef, the spare suit, the raccoon, and the Encore
    Added in
    1.65.0 (the band mended in 1.65.4, Golden Tony in 1.65.5; their kills in 1.71.1, voices 1.71.2-1.71.4, footsteps 1.71.5-1.71.6)
    Killed
    Off a few minutes, then back at its place, rebooted
    They drop
    3 to 8 iron nuggets, 1 to 3 copper ingots, 1 to 4 redstone, and often 1 to 3 Tune-In Tokens (the Encore far more)
    Years in the dark
    Suits withered, matted, crusted, worn through to the foam and in the worst to the metal, oil run from every seam and the corners of their eyes. Wide, wet, human, bloodshot eyes with a pinprick for a pupil; big flat human teeth, yellowed and gapped, and behind the costume's teeth the endoskeleton's. Arms too long, gloves too big.

    After hours

    They stand at their places, on the stage, in the kitchen, behind the curtain, in the box, in the ball pit: switched on, their heads hanging cocked to one side, jaws a little open, following you round the room, servos whining, now and then one snapping round and back. In the dark you see only the whites of their eyes, and the grins.

    Come too near, make a noise, or hit one, and it's awake: it hunts you (it sees you, hears you, searches where it lost you, drags you out of lockers). Now and then one goes off round the building on its own. Lose it and it goes back to its place. Since 1.71.1 each has its own blow, wound up first: get out of the way and it misses.

    • With the Security Card on you (the fifth night's reward), after hours they take no notice of you at all: you're staff. Not the Mended Band.
    • With the Owner's Key (all three stars), they don't stare: they turn and wave. "Hi, boss!"

    On the clock

    Punch the time clock in the security office and it's 12 AM; your shift's till 6, and each of them comes for the office its own way (below). The doors, lights, cameras, power and nights are on Tune-In Town. Leave the office more than three seconds (since 1.72.1) and tonight's cast are off the show and after you, anywhere in the building, and nothing you do hurts them while they're loose. Get back in, shut the doors on them, or keep ahead of them till 6 AM.

    Caught

    Get one in the office and it's a jumpscare: it's in your face, screaming, the world gone red. Then it's filmed: you, down on your back, and it over you, killing you its own way. Then dark, and something dragged, and Parts & Service: the golden suit slumped against the rack under one bulb, and it shoving what's left of you in, the springlocks snapping shut one after another. YOU'RE PART OF THE SHOW NOW. Then FIRED: "You're fired. (Night 1 - clock in and try it again.)" The card tells you who got you, and Hal's tip for next time.

    The twelve

    Tony Tune

    The bandleader, 130 health

    A blue-grey tabby tomcat in a top hat, his microphone on a cane. "Let's all sing!"

    Off the clock: The Spotlight ("Our STAR!"): you're lit up, glowing, and everyone in the building is called to you. His cane. On the clock: the east hall, and he moves only while nobody's watching him. When the power goes, it's Tony at the door, singing.

    Jumpscare: his cane up over his head. Kill: he clubs you with his cane. "And now... for my final number!" At the door: "Showtime, showtime... where's my audience?" Footsteps: a heavy metal step and a short servo whir. Death: his head pops off.

    Bucky Beaver

    Bass, 120 health

    "Rock on, buddy!"

    Off the clock: he gnaws through anything wooden in his way; his tail. On the clock: the west hall. Shut the door on him and he gnaws it, your power going.

    Kill: he smashes his bass down, and then his teeth. "Hold still! I'll gnaw you right down to the bone!" At the door: "Knock knock! Bucky's here to play!" Footsteps: a thick rubber foot slapping down. Death: he sits down hard on his tail and topples over.

    Lulu Lark

    Keys, 85 health

    Off the clock: she leaps at you; her Shriek (sick, slow); and when she's lost you she calls, in a child's voice from behind you: "Over here!" On the clock: east, through the kitchen, and knocking on the west door when she isn't there.

    Jumpscare: her wings flung wide. Kill: she pecks. "Sing for me, sweetie! SING!" At the door: "La la la... who's still awake?" Footsteps: metal talons clicking. Death: her wings thrash and she goes over backwards.

    Tempo the Tortoise

    Drums, 150 health, heavily armoured

    "Slow and steady..."

    Off the clock: his Drum Roll shakes the floor. Hit him from behind and you hit his shell; hurt, he goes into it. On the clock: west, a step every beat of his drum, the beats coming closer and quicker. Count them.

    Jumpscare: his head shot out of his shell. Kill: a drum roll, on you. "Keep... the... beat." At the door: "One... two... three... four..." Footsteps: a slow, floor-shaking thud and his shell's hollow knock. Death: he pulls in under his shell and it rolls over on its back.

    Rusty Rover

    The Raceway's racing hound, OUT OF ORDER, 95 health

    "Ready... set..."

    Off the clock: the fastest of them; his Sprint (into a wall, and he's dazed). On the clock: behind the Pit Stop curtain. Watch him on camera 3 or he comes out, and when he's out he RUNS for the west door (shut it; he bangs).

    Kill: his jaws, and he shakes you like a rag. "Pit stop's over, pal!" At the door: "I can smell you in there." Footsteps: four rusty metal paws trotting. Death: his legs go mid-stride, he skids and comes apart at the waist.

    Mister Channel

    The host, 95 health

    A television for a head: his smile, his static, HIS mask. "Stay tuned!"

    Off the clock: into the static, and out of it behind you; when he sees you, the dark comes over your eyes. On the clock: he hops from screen to screen (each camera goes to static), and then his face is on YOUR TV: switch it off, quick.

    Kill: his hands at your head, and he slams your face into his screen. "We'll be right back... after this message." At the door: "Stay tuned, little viewer." Footsteps: a hard plastic step crackling with static. Death: his screen flicks through its pictures to black and he folds up like a stand.

    Jack Jangle

    The jester in the Prize Corner's box, 85 health

    Off the clock: he waits in his box till you're right there: POP. Then he hops after you, box and all. On the clock: keep the music box wound (hold the button on camera 8). Let it run down and he's out.

    Jumpscare: bobbing on his stretched spring. Kill: flung on his spring, he springs down on you. "Pop goes the weasel!" At the door: "Round and round the mulberry bush..." Footsteps: his wooden box thumping and creaking. Death: his spring goes wild, he's sucked back into his box, the lid slams and the box rocks over.

    Spare Parts

    A bare endoskeleton, 65 health

    Off the clock: fast in the dark, a lunge flat along the floor. It can't stand light: a torch in your hand drives it back. On the clock: it crawls the vent, and the vent light sends it scrabbling back.

    Kill: it pulls. "Need... parts. Need... YOURS." At the door: "I can't find my face." Footsteps: bare metal skittering. Death: it falls apart, a piece at a time.

    Chef Pete

    The kitchen's walrus, 160 health, nothing knocks him back

    "Pizza's up!"

    Off the clock: he throws his pizza pans ("Order up!"); his oven-hot peel sets you alight. On the clock: the east door. Light him and he goes back to the kitchen; shut the door on him and he jams it open (for a minute, or till you reboot it on the monitor).

    Kill: his peel, and he chops. "Order up! One fresh guard, extra crispy!" At the door: "Who wants a slice, eh?" Footsteps: a heavy wet slap. Death: he clutches his chest and goes down on his face, his toque flying.

    Employee of the Month

    The old spare Tony suit, someone still in it, 110 health

    Off the clock: he grabs you and holds on (the springlocks), and talks to you in a human voice. On the clock: he never shows on the cameras, only static where he is. Listen for him: "Hello?"

    Jumpscare: his arms open for a hug. Kill: he squeezes. "Thank you for choosing Tune-In Town! Help me." At the door: "Hello? Is someone there? I just want to talk." His voice (since 1.71.3) is deeper than any of theirs, and wet: what's left of a man inside the suit, ragged, heavy breathing in every pause, his lungs giving out, his bones cracking as he moves. Since 1.71.4 he coughs, wet, hacking fits through the suit, all through the night (another way to listen for him). Footsteps: what's in his suit squelches in his boots. Death: convulsing in the springlocks, he dies on his knees.

    Tix

    The prize counter's raccoon, 55 health

    Off the clock: he snatches what's in your hand and runs. Kill him and you get it all back. On the clock: leave a door open and he steals your power and your monitor ("Something took your power - and the monitor!"); the third time, it's you.

    Kill: claws. "Ticket, please! Oh, you've only got the one..." At the door: "Got any tickets for me?" Footsteps: light paws scurrying. Death: he plays dead (spins, flops on his back, his legs in the air), and then he isn't playing.

    The Encore

    A boss, with a bar, 360 health, armoured

    All of them stitched together with his static and miles of tape. Only once someone's lasted five nights.

    Off the clock: everyone's tricks, one after another. On the clock (the sixth night): out of nowhere at any of the ways in. The screen glitches, red on the side it's coming from: shut that way NOW.

    Blow: whichever of its heads is nearest. Kill: all of it. "THE SHOW MUST GO ON." At the door: "Encore... encore...", in three of their voices at once. Footsteps: landing heavy and dragging its foot across the floor. Death: every mouth screams at once, heads flung off, a heap. It drops 4 to 8 iron ingots, 6 to 12 redstone, 8 to 14 Tune-In Tokens, 3 to 6 Signal Fragments and 1 to 2 diamonds.

    Their voices (made with ElevenLabs, since 1.71.2) come through their speaker grilles, pitched well down since 1.71.3, with metal in them, the hollow of the head, a servo under the words and the jaw clicking. Their footsteps since 1.71.6 are recorded-sounding footfalls on the tiles, four of each (five of the Employee's), so you can tell who's coming by ear.

    HIS static

    From the third night of the shift (it was the second until 1.71.1), HIS static, the First Signal's, is in one of the cast that's coming for you tonight. Never the Encore (that's all HIS anyway) and never the Employee of the Month. Nothing tells you which.

    That one comes for you no matter what. The doors don't send it back, the lights don't, being watched on the cameras doesn't; the music box won't wind with HIM in Jack, and the office TV won't stay off with HIM in Mister Channel. Get it to your door and it's a few seconds, shut or not, and it's in.

    • The clues: it twitches, in fits, its head wrenched about, its jaw snapping, its arms jerking (on the cameras too). Its eyes go red, now and then flaring, and they show on the cameras even right across the dining hall. The camera it's on stutters: static, black, back. Near the office the lights go, dark a moment, back, dark, worse the nearer it is, and you'll hear it breathing behind the door.
    • Reboot (the REBOOT button on the monitor, or R): pick one of them, and it reboots (five seconds; the cameras are only static while it does). The right one: "HIS STATIC'S GONE OUT OF ...", and it's back at its place, itself again (Have You Tried Turning It Off?). The wrong one: "... REBOOTED - IT WASN'T HIM", 5% of your power gone for nothing, and the system locked twenty seconds.

    The FIRED card always says whose HIS static was in.

    The Mended Band

    360 health, since 1.65.4. After hours, now and then (more often with someone about who thinks the card makes them safe), the band on the show stage, Tony, Bucky, Lulu and Tempo, go stiff, their eyes go red, and they come together in the middle of the stage, into each other, metal grinding through fur:

    "On the stage, the band have gone into each other. There's only one of them now - and it can see you."

    Tony's body, lurching on Tony's legs, Tempo's shell grown over its back and Tempo's face pushed out through its belly, Bucky's head out of one shoulder, Lulu's hanging upside down off the other, too many arms, all four of its mouths going. The Security Card doesn't fool it: it sees you, and comes. It roars with all four mouths and runs at you, and up close it lunges, all its arms at once. Its red eyes show on the cameras.

    After a few minutes, or with nobody left to chase, it comes apart and the band go back to their places; a shift starting, it's apart at once. Killed, all four heads scream and the band comes apart in it, the heads sagging off, the arms dropping, and down: Disbanded ("Take apart the Mended Band"). With the Owner's Key on you it never comes for you.

    Golden Tony

    The reward for all three gold stars (1.65.5): wind his music box and out he pops, a little gold Tony Tune of your own. He follows you everywhere (left behind, a long way off, in another world, after you've logged off, he's there again beside you), hums the show's song to himself, and when something hostile comes near he squeaks and points his cane at it. Nothing hurts him. Right-click him and he stays put, or comes along again. Wind the box again and he goes back in. One for each of you.
