Before 1.48.0 a parasite in a cow grew a quick brood and burst out of it. Now it doesn't. It takes the creature over, slowly, and then the creature is theirs: a ruined, writhing version of what it was, hunting everything that isn't one of them.

Anything can carry it

Any creature at all can carry it: the game's own animals, people and monsters, the golems, the Warden, the Wither and the dragon, and the Hollow's own things. The exceptions are in the case file above.

It gets into them in three ways:

  • A parasite gets in. Any of the parasite's kinds that latches on to a creature starts it.
  • A turned one hits it. What the turned hit, they put one into about a third of the time. They can put one into you too, now and then.
  • It's already out there. Once the Bloom is dead, every couple of minutes there's a one-in-three chance that a creature somewhere 24 to 80 blocks from you has one in it, already some way along. After the Watcher's visit following Signing Off it's one in two. The Wither and the dragon can catch it this way too. The Hollow's own creepypastas never do: they only catch it off one of the parasite's own.

Cutting it out

A Surgical Kit used on a creature cuts the parasite out of it any time before it starts to turn ("You cut it out of the ..."). The further along it was, the more it hurts the creature. Once it's turning, it's too late.

Turned

A turned creature is a ruined, writhing version of what it was: tendrils out of its back, and its chest (or its back) split open into a mouth of five petals round a ring of teeth. Its own mind is gone. It hunts everything that isn't one of them, the way its body lets it. The turned screech when they see you, and every one of them near comes. Village iron golems go for them.

Killed, a turned creature bursts and the parasites in it come out. Burn it and nothing does.

What it grows on them

Since 1.51.0, anything of the game's own that turns grows mutations as it goes over, whatever its body has room for: a ribcage split open on its back like jaws, whips of tendril, feelers, a second head out of its shoulder, mandibles, legs out of its sides, swollen sacs, plates of chitin, a club of it, a cannon of it. A cow, a spider, a villager, a golem, a creeper, a bat, a ghast, a slime, a Warden and the dragon each grow their own. The growths are alive: they breathe with it, twitch, lash about and strike when it strikes, and the tendril it hooks you with reaches all the way out to you. Two moves come with them:

  • Flail (anything upright): its arm gone to tendrils, three lashes in an arc.
  • Strike (anything on four legs): the head grown out of its shoulder lunges.

What comes up out of the ground for any of them (spikes, jaws, tendrils, roots) gives a moment's warning: the ground churns first. Move.

The infested bosses

The game's bosses and mini-bosses (the hard ones, three days to turn, along with anything that has 80 health or more) fight like what they were, gone wrong. They get their own bar: "Infested Iron Golem" and so on.

Infested Iron Golem

Still slams, grabs and throws

Tears the spines out of its own back and throws them at you in a fan. Impale: its arm driven into the ground and spikes of chitin up through it at you in a line. Harpoon: a barbed tendril out of its chest hooks you, reels you in and hits you.

Infested Warden

Its sonic boom gone to rot

The boom is a line of rot straight through everything, the dark after it and spores down the length of it. Tremor: it stamps and the ground tears up in rings out from it (jump them). Erupt: the tendrils on its head go down into the ground and come up under you, three times, wherever you've got to.

Infested Ravager

Gores

Puts its head down and gores you up into the air. Spray: a cone of bile out of its split head. Devour: its maw clamps on you, shakes you and flings you.

Infested Elder Guardian

Breathes spores

A cloud of spores: slow mining, poison, weakness, and it might put one in you. Leech: its tendrils into you, and it drinks and heals. Maelstrom: the water turns round it and everything's dragged in.

The others

One or two moves each

A piglin brute: Frenzy, five blows, coming on. An evoker: Jaws (chitin jaws up out of the ground in a line) and Conjure (fliers out of its robes). A breeze: Gale, a blast of wind thick with spores. A creaking: Snare, roots up round your feet while you're not looking. A sniffer: Uproot, it digs and flings it all at you. A happy ghast: Spores, bile out of the sky.

Infested Wither

Keeps how it fights

It can carry it (three days, like the rest of the hard ones). Turned, it throws brood skulls (a fan of bile), and tendrils out of its ribs drag you in.

Infested Ender Dragon

Always, since 1.61.1

The dragon is theirs from the first time anyone goes to the End (and any you bring back with the crystals; one already flying in an old world is taken the moment you're there). No days of it, no turn: violet veins under its hide, blisters along its flanks, spurs up its spine, tendrils streaming back off it and its back split open between its wings. Its bar reads "Infested Ender Dragon" and it has half as much health again. It still fights as the dragon does, and over you it shudders and rains its brood down out of the sky (void parasites, round where you stand), lobs spore bombs and sweeps its tendrils through you as it comes past low. What its body hits it can put one into. Killed, it bursts.

After Signing Off the Watcher shuts the End (see chapter five), so the dragon can't be reached until the seal is broken in chapter six.

The Hollow's great ones, turned

Since 1.51.0 the parasite can take the Hollow's eight great ones, including the Flesh's four, which it never could before. When one turns, what it was is gone: a whole new body with its own bar, its own voice and a new way of fighting. Its old fight is over (its scripts, its music, its old bar): it hunts.

The Saint Unmade

Was the Hollow Saint

It drags itself on tentacles, hunched, its chest a mouth the height of it, its head split open like a book, a scythe of chitin for one arm and whips for the other. Choir (every face it wore screams at once: it throws you back and puts out the light), Devour (the whips round you, into the mouth, chewed and spat out), Reap (the scythe), Field (tendrils up out of the ground under you and all round you), Halo (its broken halo's pods burst, bile raining down), Surge (it throws itself at you across the ground).

The Mended, Unstitched

Was the Mended

What holds the nine together now is the parasite. Hook (its chain is a tendril: the barb in you, hauled in to the scythe), Burst (every seam splits at once), Claws, Wings (Mothman's wings thrown open: every eye on them on you, the dark, the gust), Pounce.

The Gardener, Overgrown

Was the Gardener

Roots (they come up round your feet and hold you), Lash (its arm of root, the barb dragging you in), Bloom (the flower where its face was breathes out its spores), Harvest (the rusted shears, twice), Seeds (the pods on its hat burst, and spikes come up where they land).

The Broodmother, Hollowed

Was the Broodmother

Specimen Zero, Unbound

Was Specimen Zero

Off the pool floor for good, up on six legs like a spider's, Reyes bent forward off the front of it, his arms folded like a mantis's into blades of bone, his head hanging upside down off a stretched neck. The Warden, Unhoused

Was the Warden of Shelter No. 4

Its four heads up on long necks, a mouth down the whole front of the mound, six arms out of its sides, the warden on top hollowed into a chimney. Hydra (the heads strike one after another), Embrace (the arms close round everything against it and squeeze), Roll (it balls up and rolls straight at you till it hits something), Chimney (spores pour out and rain down round you in clouds), Grasp (its arms go into the floor and come up under you, holding on), Maw (the mouth opens the whole height of it, and it lunges).

The Congregation, Gutted

Was the Congregation

It's opened the bus up: ribs arching over it where the roof was, a heart the size of a car under them, its front a mouth of four flaps round a throat ringed with teeth, and it runs on all their legs like a centipede. Lamprey (it lunges, holds you in its mouth, chews and spits you out), Stampede (straight on over anything till something stops it), Ribs (they open out and slam down either side of it), Windows (the tentacles out of every window lash out and drag in whatever's beside it), Hymn (every mouth sings: slow, weak, the dark at the edges), Spill (its doors burst and parasites spill out). Get under it and you're trampled.

The rest of the Hollow's own, turned

In 1.52.0 every one of the Hollow's own that the parasite could get into got a form of its own: twenty-four more. (Things added after that, like the Listener, the Deaf King and the Tuned, have no turned form.) When one turns, what it was is gone (a whole new body, its own voice, four moves of its own), and its old ways are over (its hiding, its tricks, its scripts). It hunts. The big ones' names go on their bars.
