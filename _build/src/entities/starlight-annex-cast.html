The Starlight Revue's cast, all six and their headliner, still running their show for nobody under the Starlight Annex: porcelain over steel, satin and sequins gone grey with dust, the steel showing at the joints, eyes that light. Switched off, they stand at their places and only their heads follow you. Out of the booth, or called on in the Moon Room, they hunt you their own ways.

The cast

Madame Marigold

The fortune-teller

Torn out of her coin-op booth with the cabinet still on her, rolling. She lifts her crystal ball and marks the floor where you're going to be with a ring of light: step in it and she's behind you, her gilt hands on you. Close to, the ball comes down. On the shift: she doesn't mind the shock; get under the desk till she's gone. Her keepsake is the Tarot Deck.

Pearl

The aquarium's mermaid

Fast in the water, slow out of it. Near the edge she comes up out of the pool at you, drags you down and holds you there (hit her to make her let go). Out of the water she sings, and if you look at her you're dizzy. On the shift: she comes up the pipe by the desk. Listen for water and press its yellow button.

Roxie & Rhonda

The car-hop twins, cherry and mint

On skates. One charges, low and straight (into a wall and she's down a moment); the other circles you and throws her tray; they swap. Put one down and the other gets worse. On the shift: from night two, one up each corridor. Their keepsake is the Roller Skates.

Maestro Vincent

The bandleader, white tie and tails

When he lifts his baton the band that isn't there comes in, and every one of them plays faster. Hit him hard while his arms are up and he loses the beat. His baton jabs; his downbeat slows you. On the shift: left standing in a corridor he lifts it and everyone comes faster, so shock him first. His keepsake is the Baton.

Chef Gustave

The banquet chef

His eyes are painted on: he listens. Run, jump, shoot or talk and he comes to where he heard it and stands there listening; crouch and he loses you. Nothing there, and he bangs a pot, and the rest of them come. His cleaver. On the shift: the shock doesn't stop him; get under the desk and keep perfectly still. His keepsake is the Cleaver. Since 1.76.0 he has a range in the Supper Club and opinions about your cooking (see Gustave's kitchen), and he calls you on the payphone.

Lullabelle

The headliner, in the Moon Room

A porcelain singer in a crescent moon, riding a track round the ceiling on two long chains. When she sings you get sleepy: slower, darker, and then asleep on your feet while she comes. Crouch and cover your ears. Her moon swings down through you and lands; down on the boards she can be hurt, up on her track hardly at all. Now and then she comes down to rest. At half, the chains snap: she rocks the moon along the floor after you, calls the rest of the cast through the curtains, and her high note cracks porcelain. She has more health for every one of you. Down, and the show's over (Curtain Call). On the shift: her music box means she's dancing through the booth: hold your breath. Her keepsakes are the Music Box and the Locket.

After the Encore

Throw the switch in Gordon's office (1.75.8) and they perform the Starlight Revue one last time: the overture, each in their spotlight, Lullabelle dancing last, the bows. Afterwards they rest, heads bowed at their places, whenever nothing's on. In the Supper Club they're the band on the stage, and when they bow, the staff had better stand still.
