Description

Your shape, your size. Its skin is static, crawling. A long charcoal studio coat, old broadcast headphones round its neck with their cable over its shoulder, and a little red ON AIR lamp on its chest, lit. A plain white mask, and through its eye-holes its eyes: an eye in the dark, the whites dim grey, the rest one great black disc with points of light caught on it, always on you, narrowing as it shouts. When it moves it never hurries. When it skips, it skips like a signal, frames of it missing and its colours coming apart.

By the end of Chapter Ten he wears what his Faithful gave him (an armour formed out of them, a crown of their daggers), carries a blade drawn out of the Harbinger, and has the End's guardian's skull for a helm and its wings on his back. See the Coronation and the Second Rite.

Everything he says, word for word, is on The First Signal speaks.

ENOUGH

After the last show's credits the Watcher brings you into the Hub, the realm he made, and tells you what he can do now. He's in the middle of it ("what do you-") when the picture breaks up. The sky over the gallery tears open, and a voice comes out of it, low, huge, more than one voice at once: "ENOUGH."

He goes at the rift with both hands, pushing it shut, and a hand comes out of it and stops him. It tears the rift wide and comes through. It comes for you both. He turns to run, and it shakes its head: no. He stops. He turns back and shoves it with everything he has, and it's thrown five hundred blocks out over the static, tumbling.

It catches itself, a hand dug into the static. It tears a piece of his realm loose and stamps it to rubble, then comes back in jumps, landing on the pieces of his realm one after another, each crumbling behind it.

You go off the edge together, and falling, he shuts the realm behind you: a wall of static, slammed down. It stops at it, its hand flat on it. Hearing it, and jumping, earns ENOUGH.

What it is

Falling, the Watcher tears a portal open under you both, and you come down on an oil rig in the Dead Water (since 1.62.0). He lands beside you, crouched:

Getting there earns The First Signal. What happens on the rig is Chapter Eight.

The landing

In a world that came down from the Hub before 1.62.0 (or if the Dead Water isn't there), you land at home instead, and he says more:

He looks up: a hairline tear across the sky, static showing through it. "Somewhere past the static, the First Signal is awake - and it knows your name." Then he gives you the Remote and goes. A world like that, the next time anyone jumps off the Hub's edge, is sent on to the Dead Water: "not that way. not this time."

Past the wall

Back in the Hub (the Remote takes you there), it stood past the faint wall he shut, watching you. Now and then you heard it. The Hub won't let you sleep: "Not here. Not with it out there, past the wall."

The Broadcast

Once the Harbinger has gone through its way out of the Dead Water, the next time you're home it cuts in on you, for everyone in the Overworld: a tone, static, black. A three-part cutscene with its own score.

The wall. In the Hub, the First Signal is at its wall, both hands flat on the static. He stops. His head tilts. Then the rage: a scream of many voices through a dead channel, the wall bulging, his fists on it. He turns his back on it, lifts a hand, tears his own way open (colour bars, black) and walks into it.

His kingdom. A still black sea, a realm made of his static, and on it his castle (see his kingdom). He comes out of his tear on the platform and walks the causeway, and the water heaves: the Harbinger comes up out of it, hung on its strings, and lowers its skull to his hand.

In his war hall his servants stand round his table, a map of YOUR world on it. His hand on it, and its lights spring up all over it:

His gate opens and his creations pour out, down the causeway, into the windows, into yours. Your house is the third part: the Watcher at your door (see the Watcher). CHAPTER TEN - STAY TUNED - they're coming.

His static gets into Tune-In Town's cast too: the show went off the air the Saturday the tape played HIM.

The Refusal

The first time you come through one of his tears into his kingdom after the Broadcast (1.68.0, THE LAST BROADCAST part one), it's a cutscene. In his hall he's at his war table, his hands flat on his map, and he goes still. His head comes up. "...you." Then the rage: the scream, his fists down on his table three times, his map's lights jumping. "you came into MY house?"

His static comes up out of the sea between his platform and his castle, and he's there, through it, on his platform in front of you. His hand at your throat (since 1.72.1 holding you out at the length of his arm, your toes just off the floor): "GET. OUT." And you're thrown back out of the tear you came in by, hard, and it shuts behind you. "and STAY out." (Anyone with you goes too. Nobody's hurt by any of it.)

His static stands across his causeway the rest of that day. The next day it's gone, and so is he from his table; his war map is your world now, your house in the middle of it ringed in red, and lit on it, as they move, any of you out in it, his creations and his sites.

The Coronation

Two days after the Refusal, walk up his causeway (or wait long enough) and it's a cutscene (1.69.0). The Watcher tears his way through after you, "i couldn't let you come here alone.", and the two of you walk up to him together.

His Faithful stand round him in a ring and lift the daggers he made them, and give themselves to him with them. What they were streams into him: his armour forms out of them, piece by piece, and their daggers come together over his head: his crown. The Harbinger comes up out of the sea behind him, and he draws out of it his blade: huge, black, curved, alive, the Undertow's own teeth along its edge and an eye in it. Then he speaks:

His arena comes up out of the sea off his island's east side (its floor, tier on tier of seats, his Faithful flashing into them, his own box high on its north side), and he goes up to his box. "stay behind me. i've nothing left to fight him with - but what i have is yours."

His blade

Since 1.72.2 it's modelled whole: a sword, not a cleaver, narrower, its widest two-thirds of the way up. A thick spine, flats and a bevel down to a sharp edge, the spine dropping to a clipped point; a guard on his fist and a collar over it, two hooked quillons; the Undertow's teeth set into its edge as fangs, each leant back toward the hilt, still chattering; and its eye, an eyeball socketed right through the blade near the hilt, a red iris and a slit, lids that close over it when it blinks, looking about. When he isn't swinging it he holds it low, out to his right, its point near the floor ahead of him.

His arena

Over the bridge from his island's east side. "His arena stands off his island's east side - over the bridge. Step down onto its floor." Step down and he comes down off his box at you: "COME, THEN." His bar reads The First Signal, Crowned: 800 health, heavily armoured.

  • The sweep: his blade winds back with a whine, static crawling up it, trembling as he holds it, then round in front of him in a great cut of static, the floor torn up under it and a shockwave out across it.
  • The lunge: straight through you, leaving a streak of his afterimage.
  • His hand: he reaches for you; if it closes on you, you're lifted and flung. His static runs up his arm into you.
  • A skip: he drops a few frames, and he's behind you, already reaching.
  • Stay Tuned: he calls his creations in, and his static creeps across the floor where you stand. Get off it.
  • His scream: two rings blasted out across the arena.

Every blow that lands flashes and throws sparks, and the camera shakes with it all (by the game's screen-effects slider). The Watcher stands by the gate with no power left of his own, but every so often he gives each of you on the floor what he has: thirty seconds of something at its strongest (Strength, Speed, Regeneration, Haste, Absorption, Health Boost, Jump Boost or Fire Resistance, level V). Off its floor long enough, and he's back up in his box, whole. His arena stays: it's there whenever you come back.

A quarter of him gone: the Mast

"...enough." He's gone from the floor to his castle's roof, his hand on his mast, and every lamp on his towers goes dark, into him. The Watcher's on his knees: "no... he's taking it. all of it. mine too." His laugh: "ha. ha ha. HA HA HA HA." And the Watcher takes out the Omni: "then i'll call down something bigger." He fires it up into the sky, and the sky over his castle tears open. THE LAST BROADCAST - THE CALL - to be continued.

The Fall

Through the rip comes SENTINEL (1.70.0), all three thousand blocks of station, and stops over his castle, its gun charging at him ("SENTINEL // MAIN GUN CHARGING"). "you called THAT down on me?" At half its charge he puts everything he took from his towers up through his blade, "then watch it fall.", and his blast tears its right side off. A module of it comes down near his arena with its crew in it, and what they brought up with them. From his roof: "...your toy brought guests." "NOW. WHERE WERE WE."

The second quarter

"SENTINEL's down - and so's what was in it. He's on his arena's floor again, three-quarters of him left. The Watcher has the Omni: when he fires it, hit him." Every half-minute or so the Watcher fires the Omni into him and puts him down on one knee, his armour cracking, burning at the seams, and while he's down everything hurts him half again as much. New: he puts his blade up at the rip and bolts of his static come down where each of you is standing (red rings on the floor first: get out of them). The fight goes on to half of him.

The Forge

The Omni puts him on his knee. "...my Faithful." They come down out of their seats to him in flashes of his static, lay their hands on his armour and close its cracks, and chant their glyphs into it: his armour and his blade enchanted, glyphs crawling over them. He gets up.

He puts his blade up at what's left of SENTINEL and tears every bit of its light out of it, down into him, till it's dark. THE LAST BROADCAST - THE FORGE - to be continued.

The End

The Tearing (1.71.0): "this sea's too small for me now." He puts his blade straight up, and the sky over his arena tears open, as wide as his arena, colour bars round its edge. "HOLD ON TO SOMETHING." The whole of it is torn up out of his sea, and you with it, up into the tear.

The Arrival: his arena hangs out over the void north of the End's island, a ragged cone of rock under it, its bridge snapped off short. "...the End. he's brought it all to the End." Up out of the dark comes the End's guardian, the hive's now, and circles. "the last thing your world kept for itself." "and look what's kept it all this time." He comes down off his box.

The fight in the End: half of him gone already, he fights to a fifth of him (15%), forged now, enchanted and alight with SENTINEL's power, with the guardian strafing and diving at you over him. The Watcher still fires the Omni into him every half-minute or so. Fall off the edge into the void and his static catches you and puts you back on the floor (it hurts). Anyone who comes to his kingdom now is carried on through to his arena in the End.

The Second Rite

At 15%, a cutscene. "...not like this." Then "YOU. you'll do." He's up onto the dragon's neck as it comes down at him, his blade in it, riding it round over his arena, and it comes down on the floor. He takes its head off; tears its wings off; reaches into its chest and pulls out its heart. His Faithful come down to him and sing the second rite round him, their glyphs lit in a ring on the floor:

Its heart into his chest; its wings onto his back; and its head (since 1.72.2) drawn down small into his own as its skull forms over his, a part of his armour, piece by piece: its upper jaw over his head, a brow over each eye socket lit violet, its lower jaw under his chin, its fangs either side of his face, two pairs of horns swept back between his crown's daggers. "NOW. I AM THE END." Wings open.

On its wings

Since 1.72.0 he takes off on the dragon's wings and you fight him again, in the air over his arena: a whole new bar, The First Signal, The End, 320 health, lighter-armoured in the air. He circles over you, then:

  • A dive: reared up, his blade over his head, and down on you head first. The floor bursts where he hits it, and he's down on it a few seconds after, getting up. Hit him then.
  • A gust: three great beats of his wings, everything in front of him thrown back across the floor. Mind the edge.
  • Its breath: down low, the dragon's head on him breathes its fire in a sweep across the floor, and it burns there a while.
  • A volley: balls of its fire spat down at you one after another, bursting where they land.
  • Seized (once he's lost a third): he swoops down and carries you up in his hand, crushing you, and throws you down. Hurt him enough while he's got you and he lets go.
  • His static (once he's lost two-thirds): bolts down out of the sky where you stand.

The Watcher's Omni still knocks him out of the sky now and then (down on the floor, wide open). A bow, a crossbow or a trident helps. Brought down to nothing, he falls out of the air, turning over and over, onto where the rite was, and the Watcher gets up.

Taken

The Watcher lets the Omni fall and gets up off his knees. "the End was never yours." The Watcher's static throws him back off his feet; he draws his blade back, trembling, and swings it, and the Watcher catches it; and the Watcher puts his beam (a real beam of shadow since 1.72.0) into his heart: "i've watched you since the first night. i know what you are." Then his Faithful seize him. A seal opens behind him, black, ringed in their glyphs.

They're gone into it with him, and it's shut. The Watcher, on one knee: "...they took him. somewhere even i can't see." "go home. while there's still a home to go to." Then the Rebirth, and the card: THE LAST BROADCAST - THE SECOND RITE - to be continued. His arena's gone from his sea after it, and so is he.
