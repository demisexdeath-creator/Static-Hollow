Static Hollow has its own advancements tab, added in 1.34.0. Its root reads Static Hollow: Something has been watching you. There were 89 advancements under it in 1.72.2, from the end of chapter four to Tune-In Town and chapter ten, plus one for every chapter's gear, and since 1.74.0 45 more for the Easter eggs. Spoilers, obviously.

Each one shows its frame as the game draws it: a task (square), a goal (rounded) or a challenge (spiked; drawn doubled here). 14 are hidden until you earn them. The icons are the mod's own textures where the advancement uses one of its items.

The tab

  • Static Hollow

    Something has been watching you.

The root's own icon in game is the Rosemont Device.
