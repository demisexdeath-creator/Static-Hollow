The shader packs are downloaded unmodified; the "-StaticHollow" copies are made on each player's own computer and never shipped. What the newer mods do in the game is on cold, thirst and the world.

Ideas the README credits

These gave the mod an idea. In each case the README says nothing of theirs is used.

  • Tectonic (MIT): lava glowing at the very bottom of the deep oceans. The deep is the game's own terrain, stretched.
  • Olaf's Relatively Placed Blocks: small blocks sharing one space.
  • Tough As Nails: the cold and the thirst.
  • Subnautica (1, 2 and Below Zero), Alex's Caves and Thalassophobia: the Dead Water's seas. Every creature, model, texture and sound there is Static Hollow's own.

Tools

  • Blockbench for the creatures' models. The project files come separately as StaticHollow-Blockbench.zip.
  • Real-ESRGAN, an AI upscaler, for the optional HD packs (and since 1.61.0 the chapter gear in them).

  • Every story detail, item description, note and line of dialogue comes from the mod's own files (its README, language file, data and source), version 1.63.3.

The home page's scene is rebuilt from the mod's own main-menu layers, and the item and advancement icons are the mod's own textures. The cover art is the mod's CurseForge image.
