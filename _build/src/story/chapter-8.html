Off the edge of the Hub, with the First Signal behind a wall of static, the fall goes somewhere else. The Watcher tears a portal open under you both, and you come down onto an oil rig in a dead green sea that goes on until it doesn't.

Chapter Eight arrived in 1.62.0, and 1.63.0 to 1.63.22 filled its sea. Since 1.63.5 it's one map, the same in every world, and since 1.63.6 each of its places stands there once, rebuilt bigger, and since 1.63.7 with people in it. The title card reads CHAPTER EIGHT, DEAD WATER, the deep is listening. Its realm is the Dead Water, a dimension of its own. When Orpheus has all three of her modules, it goes on into Chapter Nine.

Getting there

In a world that reaches the Hub in 1.62.0 or later, it follows straight on from ENOUGH: you jump, and falling, he tears a blue portal of signs open under you. A world that already had Chapter Seven's landing at home gets here the next time someone takes the Remote to the Hub and goes off its edge. He's there, falling beside you:

Either way you come out over the rig's helipad and land, and he lands after you, crouched (Dead Water).

He gives you the Remote, and it breaks in your hand, turning into the Broken Remote (press it and "It sparks. The warm button's cold. Nothing."). Then he goes over the rail into the sea. The chat says what you need: "Chapter Eight: Dead Water. The Remote's broken - mend it with a Static Leviathan's Signal Organ and a copper ingot. They live deep."

The rig

An oil platform standing alone in the Dead Water, four great legs of rusted steel going down into the black. He built it a long time ago, he says, and it's been waiting with its lights on and its larders full:

  • The cellar deck: the moon pool (a square of open water with a ladder down into it), the dive locker, the pumps, the pens (chickens, sheep, cows and a pig, let out when you arrive), the boat dock below it with two boats, and since 1.63.0 the sub bay off the dock, where the submarine Orpheus lies in its clamps, with its console on the catwalk beside her berth.
  • The main deck: the quarters (eight beds), the mess and its kitchen, the infirmary, the workshop, the stores and the greenhouse (crops under glass, water channels, a composter).
  • The upper deck: the control room, the radio room, the armoury, and the helipad you came down on.
  • The derrick in the middle with red lights at its crown, two cranes, the flare stack out over the sea that never goes out, and the lifeboats.

You're stranded until the Remote is mended. The rig's beds set your spawn but you can't sleep in them ("You can't sleep here. The sea won't let you."). Die out here and the sea brings you back to the quarters: "The sea brings you back. Nothing leaves the Dead Water while the Remote's broken." You can't lose the Broken Remote either; it's always still in your pocket.

The rig's chests (the armoury, the dive locker, the mess, the stores, the sub bay) have what you'll need for the sea: the Harpoon Gun (hold to pump, let go to fire, in water or out; it reels you to the catch or the catch to you) and Harpoon Bolts, Marine Flares (a minute of red light, even underwater), Rig Rations and Air Tanks (a dozen breaths). Thirst and the cold (also new in 1.62.0) follow you here, so keep a canteen filled too. All of it is on Items and blocks.

The Watcher's chart

A little after you come down, the Watcher presses a rolled chart into your hands:

The Watcher's Chart shows every water in its own colour and named, the floor's depths shaded and contoured, and the Black Below hatched in red as THE DEAD ZONE. Since 1.63.5 it covers the whole sea, out past the edge all round, and since 1.63.6 every one of the sea's places is on it as a red cross with its name. Held, it's a map of the stretch you're in (a thousand blocks a square), with you as an arrow, the others down there with you, and the rig. Use it to unroll the whole chart: scroll to zoom, drag to look round, R to come back to you. Point at a water and you get his note on it. A Map, a Glow Ink Sac and a Prismarine Shard make another.

One sea

Since 1.63.5 the Dead Water is laid out like one sea, the same in every world. The rig stands in the middle of the Grey Shelf. Round it is a ring of the shallow waters, one of each: the Lantern Fields, the Molar Reef, the Haemal Reach, the Drowned Mycelia and the Rime Shelf. Round that is a ring of the deeper ones: the Wireweed, the Brine Sink, the Ember Vents, the Carrier Wave and the Ossuary Flats. Their edges wander, and the floor slopes from one to the next. About three thousand blocks out the floor slopes down to the edge of everything and falls away: the Black Below, the void, all the way round, for ever. (A world that had already been to the Dead Water keeps the sea it had where it had been; the new map is where its sea is still new.)

Since 1.63.6 there's one of each of the sea's places, standing in its own water's own stretch of the map, every one of them rebuilt bigger, to go into. /locate structure finds each one from anywhere in the Dead Water (since 1.63.18 it sets you down in the water right beside it), and the Watcher's chart marks every one. Since 1.63.21 the floor round each is levelled to its own, so none stands in a pit or on a plinth.

The crews

Since 1.63.7 the places have their own people. The Deep Hands still live and work down there in the old diving dress: the round copper helmet with a face behind its lit front glass, the brass corselet, lead on their boots, a big spanner. Each working place has its own crew in its own colours: the Ember Crew at the pumping station, Lantern Station's Keepers, Sink Station's Researchers, the Signal Keepers at the mast, the Salvagers in the Wreck Field and the Castaways at the Lifeboat Graveyard (and since 1.63.11 the Rime Crew, who hunt the frostjaws near their camp with harpoon guns). They walk from job to job, wave when you first come near and never go for you, except the Salvagers: hit one and the lot of them come for you with their spanners. Right-click one and it talks to you. Since 1.63.10 the Ember Crew trade, too.

Not everyone who went down stayed themselves. The Drowned keep the Drowned Heart: the crew that went down to see what it was, their dress split and the Flesh coming out through it. The Sporeborn hang on their strings under the Drowned Grove's caps, and the Grove talks to you through them. The Stretched lie round the research station, the lab's divers as the Enzyme left them, until you come near. See the bestiary.

What the water does to you

  • The Haemal Reach (1.63.9): Enzyme 1-2418 is in all of its water, and without the UV dress it gets into you. At 50 "You want to go to the Heart. You don't know why.", and it pulls you there. A flash of the UV Lamp burns a fifth of it out of you and the lab's Enzyme Inhibitor half; the UV Dive Suit keeps it out.
  • The Drowned Mycelia (1.63.13): breathe its water and its spores build up in you, until "The Grove has you." A sealed helmet keeps most of it out, Orpheus all of it, and a Spore Tonic burns sixty of it out of you.
  • The deep (1.63.10): below about y -100 the sea crushes you, more the deeper you go. The Ember Deep Suit, made from what the pumping station's crew trade, keeps it off, and so does Orpheus.

What the Grove, the Grove Heart, the Enzyme and Orpheus say to you down there is on Tapes and broadcasts.

Orpheus

The submarine Orpheus waits in its own sub bay off the rig's dock (1.63.0; it's added to rigs that were already up). Since 1.63.2 it's a real submarine thirteen blocks long, with a cabin you walk about in, for up to four people. Climb in by its hatch and down the ladder ("The helm is up in the bow, under the dome; the bunks, lockers, galley and battery are aft. Walk about her, and use whatever you're looking at."). Climbing out (hold crouch) puts you down off her side; since 1.63.22 her cabin is dry air, and the sea starts at her glass.

  • At the bow, under the dome: the helm (take it and drive; crouch to step away), the floodlight switches and the sonar console.
  • Down its length: the chart table (the Watcher's chart), two bunks (lie down to heal, once every two minutes), lockers (27 slots, kept with the sub), a workbench, the galley (its hot plate cooks what you hold), the battery bank (charged with copper ingots, redstone or a block of redstone), the repair board (iron ingots patch the hull and the dome's cracks) and the motor.
  • Driving: W/S thrust, A/D turn, jump to rise, sprint to dive. Use switches the floodlights (they light the water ahead, and draw things that love light, and things that hunt it), attack works its claw, and swap-hands pings the sonar. A submarine's sonar shows where a Mouth of the Floor lies.
  • Depth: it's rated to 183 m down. Past that the hull creaks and the glass cracks, and at nothing it implodes.

Her upgrade bay

Since 1.63.8, use her motor (aft, in the engine room) and her upgrade bay opens: five mounts for modules, to fit, swap and take out as you like. What's in it is what she has, and the motor tells you her rating. Three modules matter for the story:

  • Pressure Plating (iron blocks, Needle Teeth, Shroud Hide and obsidian, or found down there): no crush depth at all. She can go as deep as the sea goes.
  • The Depth Module (280 m): she's rated to 280 m with it. There's one in the parts crate on the sub bay's catwalk, and it can be made from iron blocks, copper blocks, tinted glass and a block of redstone.
  • The UV Field Module (1.63.9; four UV Emitters, copper, glowstone and redstone): her floodlights burn violet and keep the Enzyme off her, and burn it out of her crew. Without it, in the Haemal Reach, she goes to flesh: her hull warm, then veined, then all of it, eyes and mouths, breathing. She talks to you, and begs. All the way, and she keeps you.

Fit all three and the sea answers: see what comes next.

Lost her? Since 1.63.21 the sub bay's console, on the catwalk by her berth, tells you where she is: in her clamps (her hull and power), out at sea (where she was last heard from, and how far), or nothing heard from her at all, and then the bay's welders build her again in her clamps, a minute's work, nothing asked of you. Use it crouching and she's scrapped where she lies and built again. Since 1.66.6, when she's lost her emergency locker blows free with everything that was in her lockers and the modules out of her bay, and floats at the top of the sea with its lamp blinking until someone empties it.

The Static Heart

Out past the edge, on the floor of the abyss, lies the Canticle's anchor (rebuilt in 1.63.8): a hundred blocks of it, its chain rising two hundred blocks out of the dark and broken off, and beside it the Canticle herself, her bow half sunk, her name on her side, a lamp still lit in her cabins. All round are the bones of what the Undertow has eaten: whales, a leviathan's skull with its jaws open, fish, divers. Its crown is hollow (go in on its west side), and in the hollow is the Static Heart: "Warm, and beating - to no rhythm of its own. There's a picture in it, of nothing, always moving". "Keep it. Something is going to be made with it".

The Watcher, asleep

He went over the rail when you came down. Since 1.63.21 you can find where he went: straight down from where he went into the sea off the rig, he's there at the bottom, curled up, asleep, floating in a bubble of his own static with a faint light round him in the dark. Nothing gets into the bubble, and nothing you swing at it, throw at it or fire at it gets through: "The bubble gives a little - then pushes you back out. He doesn't stir." He doesn't wake until the Turning.

The Static Leviathan

What you need lives deep. The Signal Organ comes from the Static Leviathan, a boss with its own bar, forty-five blocks long since 1.63.2, with fangs, tusks and its ribs showing through its rotted hide. It charges, it swallows you whole, and it sings on the Remote's frequency. Go more than forty blocks under the surface, anywhere but the Black Below, and now and then one comes up out of the dark after you. (Back home they live in the Overworld's new deep oceans too, down in the Hadal Dark and the Trench.)

The Black Below

Don't take the little boat over it. Since 1.63.5 the void past the edge is the Undertow's: go out over it at all, swimming, in Orpheus or in a boat on top of it, and stay, and something notices (sooner the deeper you are). A rumble from below, and it comes up out of the dark, a thing three thousand blocks long whose head is a flower of jaws. Get back over the edge and it lets you go.

You can see it in the dark now, shaders or not: lines of cold light run the length of its body, a chevron of light over its back every twenty blocks, and its eyes glow, ringed in cold light, and stay on you. It doesn't come straight at you. It stalks you ("Something vast is keeping pace with you, out in the dark."), off one shoulder; every half minute or so it slides up into the corner of your eye and holds there, and soon after it finds you it makes a pass right under you. It learns you (since 1.63.3), and tells you so:

It has learned how you move.

It saw you run for the surface. It will be waiting under you.

It has learned your light. It keeps out of it.

It knows you now.

What it learns, it keeps. It eats when it's hungry ("It is hungry."). Only when it's starving does it roar and come for you; before that, if a lost whale is near, it eats the whale instead ("It has fed. For now."). Since 1.63.5 there's always a whale about: one comes out of the dark ahead of you within seconds of you going out over the void, the reef on its back glowing warm, and keeps near you, singing. When the Undertow comes, it roars (a submarine's glass cracks and its power dies), lunges and swallows you whole. Run for the surface and it breaches after you, hundreds of blocks into the sky.

Getting home

Craft the Broken Remote, the Static Leviathan's Signal Organ and a copper ingot together (Back On Air): "The Remote works again. Press it: the Hub (and in the Hub, home). Crouch and press it: back to the rig."

The Dead Water stays yours to come back to. The rig and each of the twelve waters have music of their own.
