You come home, and it cuts in on you: a tone, static, black. In the Hub, the First Signal takes his hands off the wall. He doesn't need it any more. He has a kingdom of his own, and a map of your world on his table.

Chapter Ten arrived in 1.64.0, and 1.64.1, 1.64.2 and 1.67.0 made it worse. The title card reads CHAPTER TEN, STAY TUNED, they're coming, and the chapter's own music is Stay Tuned. From 1.68.0 its story goes on in The Last Broadcast.

The Broadcast

Once the Harbinger has gone through its way out, the next time you're home (back at your bed or your spawn in the Overworld, or home by the Remote), it cuts in on you, for everyone in the Overworld: a tone, static, black. A cutscene in three parts, with its own score (On Air and Get Ready).

The wall

In the Hub, the First Signal is at the wall the Watcher shut on him, both hands flat on the static. He stops. His head tilts. Then the rage: a scream of many voices through a dead channel, the wall bulging, his fists on it. He turns his back on it, lifts a hand, tears his own way open, colour bars and black, and walks into it.

His kingdom

A still black sea, in a realm made of his static: it crawls in the walls of the world, far off all round. On the sea stands his castle of black static-stone, dead television sets in its walls, red lamps up its towers and its mast. Under the water, all round, are the windows: every realm he's touched, each one lit, a cable running from each to his castle. He comes out of his tear on the platform and walks the causeway, and his Faithful go down on their faces as he goes by.

The water heaves, and the Harbinger comes up out of it, hung on its strings. He speaks to it:

It lowers its skull to his hand. In his war hall his servants stand round his table, a map of your world on it. He puts his hand on it, and its lights spring up all over it.

His gate opens, and his creations pour out, down the causeway and into the windows. Into yours. The whole place is on his kingdom; everything he says is on The First Signal speaks.

Your house

Your door bangs open (the nearest door to where you are; with none near, he's simply there, since 1.72.1 somewhere he can actually stand): the Watcher, out of breath.

CHAPTER TEN, STAY TUNED, they're coming. That's Stay Tuned ("See what he did after the Hub - and what he let out"), and the chat says: "Chapter Ten: Stay Tuned. His creations are out - they see you, they hear you. Hide: lockers, under beds, crouched in the tall grass."

The invasion

The first of them come for you a few seconds after, near home. Every night after (and any time you're down in the caves) the broadcast goes out round you, a tone far off ("...the broadcast goes out."), and they come: more each night you've lived through, up to ten, out of the dark and out of your sight. By day there are Lookalikes, near villages and herds.

They're his own, nobody else's: the Longplay, a person drawn out like a chewed-up tape, eleven feet tall, that pauses when you watch it; the Hanger, folded the wrong way, up walls and over roofs; the Lookalike, a villager, a cow, a pig, a sheep, a chicken or the Watcher, a few moments behind itself; and ERR, the broken script, a body in slices with errors in your chat. Over them are his three servants, bosses each: the Announcer, the Cameraman and the Stagehand. Since 1.64.1 they hunt together: one sees you, its head snaps round, it shrieks, and every one of his for thirty blocks comes. All of them are on his creations and servants.

They see you and they hear you. Each has a cone of sight, shorter in the dark, much shorter if you're crouched, and crouched in tall plants it can't make you out past a few blocks. They hear you sprint, jump and land, break and place blocks, open doors, fight and eat, and come to look. The nearer one is to finding you, the faster and louder your heartbeat, and his static creeps in at the edges of your eyes. Since 1.67.0 they climb walls after you if you're above them (not his servants), and stuck, they claw through wood, glass, earth, leaves or wool (stone and iron hold them).

Since 1.67.0 each night of Chapter Ten has its own programme, never last night's again, told as the night comes in: TONIGHT ON CHANNEL ZERO.

The first days

Since 1.67.0 he doesn't leave the first ones to chance:

  • The first time you step out of doors by day after the Broadcast, his transmitter comes up out of the ground right in front of you: "Out there the ground splits open - and something comes up out of it, red lamps climbing its mast. His."
  • The first time you're down in a cave, his burrow is eaten out of the rock near you: "Somewhere in the rock near you, something is eating its way through. You can hear it chewing toward you."
  • On the third night (two of his nights lived through), the first time you're out of doors in the dark, his first set goes up out there in front of you (always, since 1.72.2): "Out there in the dark, studio lights come on, one after another. Someone's built a set - and there are people standing about on it, waiting."
  • On the third day, while you're home, three knocks at your door. Someone's left a set on your step, facing in, switched on, showing static till the door's opened. Then it's the flyer: see Tune-In Town.

His sites

His sites come through into your world after it, each kept by one of his servants, and each with a Broadcast Core. Break the core and the site goes off the air ("...and off the air."): its lamps go out, it calls nothing more, and the core gives up what it was burning. Their chests hold the best of what he brought. A keeper you've killed doesn't come back.

Hiding

The chat told you how: lockers, under beds, crouched in the tall grass.

  • The Steel Locker (iron and bars): get in and pull the door to. You see out through its vents and nothing sees in.
  • Under a bed: crouch and use any bed.
  • Crouched in tall plants (grass, ferns, bushes, leaves, crops): they can't make you out past a few blocks.

One that's searching may come and stand at your hiding place, listening, sniffing. If it heard you in there, or saw you go in, it tears it open and drags you out; make a sound and it has you.

Blackout is what you fight him with, the strongest gear there is: Signal Fragments from his creations, Blackout Shards from his servants, his cores and the blackout ore his static seeps into deep down. The Blackout Blade's Signal Cut tears at everything of his in front of you; its tools are silent; each piece of its armour makes you quieter, and the whole set is silent, with Lights Out (tap sneak twice) to throw everything hunting you down and vanish for ten seconds. See Gear for every chapter.

The Watcher at your house

He stays. Anything of the First Signal's that comes near your house, the Watcher strikes down. He can be fooled by a Lookalike, though, and on THE IMPOSTORS something else comes knocking wearing him.

A way in

Since 1.64.2 there's a way into his kingdom. Break the Broadcast Core in one of his sites and his signal's torn open where it stood, a tear like the one he comes through, colour bars rolling in it, its edges crackling, for a minute: "Where it stood, his signal's torn open. It won't stay that way long." Step into it and you're on his platform at the end of the causeway, his castle across the black water: "His kingdom. Behind you, the way back." Behind you is a redder tear, the way back to just where you were. While you're his guest his realm doesn't throw you out (His Guest), but "You can't sleep here. Not in his kingdom."

Nothing's quiet there. His Faithful, his congregation in robes of a red so dark it's nearly black, keep his rites in circles of candles; their hoods turn after you, and hurt one and every one of them for 24 blocks runs at you. Whispering just behind you, footsteps coming up behind you and stopping, a music box winding down, someone crying through the walls. Since 1.67.0 his sea goes down two hundred blocks, and the windows stand on rock pillars up out of the deep.

The first time you go through, he notices. That's where The Last Broadcast begins.

Tune-In Town

Tune-In Town Pizza & Playhouse's show went out live on Channel 6 every Saturday from its own stage, its cast of animatronics running off reel-to-reel showtapes, till the Saturday the tape played him. It shut. Now he's opening it again.

It's a secret, an Easter egg (1.65.0 to 1.65.5, furnished and voiced in 1.71), and it doesn't wait for this chapter: since 1.65.3 it can turn up any time after your first day. Once in a long while an Old Set that's switched on stops showing static and shows a yellowed HELP WANTED flyer held up close to the camera, or one dark night you'll come on a set left on in the grass a little way in front of you, showing it. In Chapter Ten it also comes to your door on the third day. Look at the set and it clicks off, and the flyer's in your hand: it gives you the address. What's left of the old place has a steel door at the back by the stage that isn't rotten at all: STAFF ONLY. Through it is Studio B, the place as it was on television, and right at the back, the security office.

The first time you walk in there, a WARNING comes up (read the photosensitivity warning before you go): flashing lights, flickering screens, loud noises and jumpscares, and if you go on, you're kept there and can't play the story or the rest of the game until you've survived five nights of the night shift. Twelve of the cast come for the office, each its own way, and from the third night (since 1.71.1; it was the second) his static is in one of them. Hal, the old manager, talks you through it on the office TV: his tapes are word for word on Tapes and broadcasts. The place is on Tune-In Town, and the twelve on Tune-In Town's cast.
