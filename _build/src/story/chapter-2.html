When the Hollow Saint falls, the floor where it fell tears open. The creepypastas were keeping something in; now there's a hole, and it's been widening ever since.

The Breach

A red wound stands up out of the ground: black inside, its edges burning, red lightning crawling off it, ash drifting round it and the air pulling in. In a world where the Saint already fell before this was added, one tears open near you soon after you play. Walk into it.

The Void

Since 1.25 the Breach lets out into its own dimension: black all round, no ground and no sky, a fog the colour of a bruise, ash and embers drifting, a drone that never stops, and things moving out in the dark. Now and then, far out, two great red eyes open and look at you. You can't sleep out here.

You come out on one torn-off island: pale dead moss over an upturned crag of earth and rose stone, crystals and roots hanging off it, dead trees leaning over the edge, and the Breach Altar's shrine on top. Four more pieces of the Hollow hang in the dark round it, the Drift, each with a chest and a page, all reachable on foot:

  • The Torn Room (east, over a sagging rope bridge): the corner of a house ripped off at the floor, the bed unmade, a table laid for two, a music box playing somewhere.
  • The Fallen Graves (west, over stepping stones): a lump of graveyard and a little mausoleum, its coffins sticking out of the earth underneath.
  • The Mast (south): the Broadcast's radio mast, a red aviation light blinking on top that you can see from all over the Void, and a shed with a TV still on.
  • The Nest (north): the carcass of something huge, a ring of standing stones round a black pool, sculk over everything. The lampreys come up out of the pool.

If you fall, your fall slows as if the dark thickened, and a hand bigger than a man comes up out of it, closes round you (it hurts), and sets you back down on the island you fell from. Out here you'll also meet Driftlights, harmless jellyfish of pale flesh that light the islands, and Lampreys, eyeless eels with a glowing lure that bite and try to drag you out over the dark.

The Breach Altar

The Breach Altar is the Forsaken Altar's other half: a platform of pale rose stone, spires of pink crystal, pink candles round a pink altar with a torn eye carved in it. Wake it with an empty hand and everything turns purple: every crystal, every flame, every vein in the stone.

The killers and the things from the woods

The new nine are five killers and four cryptids. Like the creepypastas they stalk first, at the treeline, across the water, overhead, and only come for you once they've watched long enough or you hit them. Each drops its own item and its head (always on the first kill, then 10% plus Looting). The Watcher has something to say the first time you kill each.

The Murder Mansion

The Watcher tells you where they live. The Murder Mansion is a three-storey Second Empire house under a black mansard roof, with a turret, a widow's walk and a chimney still smoking, in its own railed yard: a fountain of blood, dead hedges, things hanging from dead trees, the family plot. Inside are a foyer with twin staircases, a dining hall laid for guests who are only skulls now, a butcher's kitchen, a nursery, a bathtub full of blood, the red room and the attic.

The house's papers are where they were left. Read them in order and you learn what the Hale family did: the housekeeper's accounts, the butcher's order book, a letter never sent, a nursery rhyme that counts the nine, the guest list, a surveyor's notes, a note from the glasshouse, the red room's instructions, and the Watcher's own pages on the mending, open on the library desk. They're all on the lore page.

Through the Harvest Doors is the Trophy Hall: chains and hooks out of the dark, trophies on every wall, and the Mending Altar inside a horseshoe of nine trophy pedestals.

The Mended

Put one head from each of the new nine on the trophy pedestals and right-click the Mending Altar with an empty hand. The Watcher comes down from the ceiling and sews. It takes about thirty seconds, and nothing can hurt the result until it stands and roars with all nine voices.

The Mended is four blocks tall, 750 health, with three phases: Agony, Aggression and Iron Will. When it dies it comes apart and the nine go up out of it. It leaves the Mended Heart and the Rusted Garden Key, with iron, chains and one of each of the nine's drops.

What you can make

  • Six weapons: the Bunnyman's Fire Axe (Bridge Toll), Hook and Chain (Reel), the Hogshead's Tenderizer (Tenderize), the Porcelain Needle (Unseen), the Devil's Glaive (Hellfire) and the Rending Claws (Frenzy).
  • Three armour sets: Butcher's Garb (Slaughterhouse), Mothwing (Night Flight) and Braxton Shell (Braxton Mist).

Recipes and powers are on the items page.
