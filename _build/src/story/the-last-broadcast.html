You came into his house. He throws you out of it. Two days later he crowns himself, pulls a blade out of the Harbinger, and lifts an arena up out of his sea to fight you in. The Watcher calls down the biggest thing he can, and the First Signal takes it.

The Last Broadcast is the rest of Chapter Ten, in four parts: part one, the Refusal (1.68.0); part two, the Coronation, his arena and the Call (1.69.0); part three, the Fall, the second quarter and the Forge (1.70.0); and part four, the Tearing, the End's guardian, the Second Rite and the Rebirth (1.71.0), with the fight on the dragon's wings added in 1.72.0. From part two on, each part runs straight on into the next, and it ends, for now, on to be continued. It all happens in his kingdom, and at the end in the End. Nobody is hurt by any of its cutscenes, and anyone else there sees them too. Its fight has its own music, The Last Broadcast.

Part one: the Refusal

The Refusal

The first time you come through one of his tears into his kingdom after the Broadcast (or if you're there already), it's a cutscene. His castle, across the black water. In his hall he's at his war table, his hands flat on his map, and he goes still. His head comes up.

The First Signal, at his table

...you.

The scream; his fists down on his table three times, his map's lights jumping

you came into MY house?

His static comes up out of the sea between his platform and his castle, and the castle's gone behind it. He's there, through it, on the platform in front of you, his hand at your throat

GET. OUT.

He holds you out at arm's length, your toes just off the floor (since 1.72.1), and throws you back out of the tear you came in by, hard, the way the Watcher threw him out of the Hub. It shuts behind you, and in chat, in dark red: "and STAY out." Anyone else in his kingdom with you is thrown out with you ("Thrown out of his kingdom.").

His static

For the rest of that day his static stands across his causeway. Come back through a tear and there's no getting past it; it throws you back: "His static stands across his causeway. He's shut you out - for today."

His map of your world

The next day his static's gone, and he isn't at his table. His war map isn't the one it was any more: it's your world, your house in the middle of it ringed in red, two thousand blocks either side of it and more along the length of his table, worked out from your world's own ground (the sea and the lakes, the rivers, the woods, the deserts, the snow, the heights in contour), his red grid over it every five hundred blocks. And lit on it as they move: any of you out in your world, his creations, his sites.

Your house. On his map.

His war map isn't the one it was. It's your world - your house in the middle of it, two thousand blocks all round it - lit wherever any of you are, wherever his creations are, wherever his sites stand. And he isn't at his table.

His Faithful are at their rites as they were (and can be killed).

Part two: the Coronation

The third day

Two days after the Refusal, come through to his kingdom and something enormous is in the black water round his castle: "Something enormous is moving in the water round his castle." It's the Harbinger, the Undertow taken by his static, up out of the sea, over and down again, now here, now there, well out from his castle and never over his causeway.

The Coronation

Walk up his causeway (or wait long enough) and it's a cutscene. The Watcher tears his way through after you: "i couldn't let you come here alone." The two of you walk up to him together.

The First Signal

...both of you.

good. you'll want to see this.

His Faithful stand round him in a ring and lift the daggers he made them, and give themselves to him with them. What they were streams into him: his armour forms out of them, piece by piece, and their daggers come together over his head as his crown. The Harbinger comes up out of the sea behind him, and out of it he draws his blade: huge, black and alive, the Undertow's own teeth set along its edge as fangs, and an eye in it near the hilt that blinks and looks about. Then he speaks, to the Watcher first and then to you:

The First Signal, crowned

you've gone too far, Watcher.

every code. every rule. you broke them all.

so the order now is your erasure.

every world you made. every realm. every creature.

i'll rip that poor void heart out of you - and keep it.

and that will be the end of time.

the most powerful creation. pulling the strings - forever.

as i should be. and you... i have no use for you.

don't make me angrier, and i'll give you a whole new world.

but i know what you want.

YOU'LL BOTH FAIL.

His arena comes up out of the sea off his island's east side: its floor, tier on tier of seats, his Faithful flashing into them, his own box high on its north side. He goes up to his box.

"He's crowned. His arena's up out of the sea - step down onto its floor and he'll come down to you."

His arena

It's over the bridge from his island's east side. Step down onto its floor and he comes down off his box at you ("COME, THEN."), crowned, with his blade, under a bar of his own: The First Signal, Crowned. He sweeps it round in front of him and lunges straight through you; there's his hand, his skips, his static on the floor, his scream, and his creations called in ("STAY TUNED."). Since 1.71.2 the blade winds back with a whine, static crawling up it, before it comes round in great cuts that tear up the floor.

The Watcher stands by the gate with no power left of his own, but every so often he gives each of you on the floor what he has: thirty seconds of something at its strongest, level V (Strength, Speed, Regeneration, Haste, Absorption, Health Boost, Jump Boost or Fire Resistance). "The Watcher gives you [it] V - for thirty seconds."

Stay off its floor long enough and he's back up in his box, whole. His arena stays: it's there whenever you come back. His Faithful sometimes drop the daggers he made them (one in five), a quick blade. The fight itself is on the First Signal.

The Mast

Take a quarter of him and it's a cutscene. "...enough." He's gone from the floor to his castle's roof, his hand on his mast, and every lamp on his towers goes dark, into him. The Watcher's on his knees: "no... he's taking it. all of it. mine too." His laugh: "ha. ha ha. HA HA HA HA." And the Watcher takes out the Omni.

He fires it up into the sky, the call, and the sky over the castle tears open. (In 1.69.0 it stopped there, on a card: THE LAST BROADCAST, THE CALL, to be continued. Since 1.70.0 there's no card: the Fall goes straight on from it.)

Part three: the Fall

The Fall

The call goes straight on into the Fall (in a world where the call was already made, it plays the next time any of you is in his kingdom). THE LAST BROADCAST, THE FALL, part three. The sky over his castle rips open right across the north, black in it and his colour bars round its edge, and through it comes SENTINEL. All of it this time, not just its gun: three thousand blocks of station, its hub, its gun under it, two habitat rings on their spokes, its truss out to its wingtips, its solar wings, radiators and modules, a docking spine with ships on it, its windows lit and its lamps blinking. It stops over his castle, and its gun charges at him, its read-out counting up along the top of the picture: "SENTINEL // MAIN GUN CHARGING".

The First Signal

you called THAT down on me?

The Watcher: "...come on. come on." At half its charge, he puts everything he took from his towers up through his blade

then watch it fall.

His blast tears SENTINEL's right side off: "HULL BREACH // SECTIONS 4-9 // CONTAINMENT LOST // HOLD B OPEN". "no. no, no, no -", says the Watcher. That half comes down burning into his sea far out (the sea goes up where it hits, and it lies there after, its broken end up out of the water), and a module of it comes down near, east of his arena.

SENTINEL's wreck and crew

The module stays: real blocks, lying tilted in the sea, its hatch end up out of the water and its far end torn open, scorched, the parasites' resin in it, its windows, deck and lamps (some out), bits of its solar wing and a girder round it, fires on its back. Its crew's chest is still in it, with SENTINEL's crew log among what's in there.

Its crew, the SENTINEL Crew, astronauts in their white suits and gold visors, climb out of its hatch onto its hull, their radio breaking up:

SENTINEL- to anyone. we're down. we are DOWN.

SENTINELwhere are we? that's not... that's not our sky.

SENTINEL...something's moving in the hold.

SENTINELstay back. STAY BACK.

SENTINEL brought Blackwater's parasites up with it, and they're out of the hold and onto the crew, one after another. The last of them backs off to the end of the hull, and the parasites stop, and the Chorus's last voice speaks through them: "...one voice left." "it will do." They pour into him, and the hive comes up through him: "we hear you, signal."

The Harbinger comes up out of the sea beside the wreck and swallows him whole, and the hive gets into it (its bones go violet). "what... what was THAT?", says the Watcher. A door of the hive's chitin opens on the air out past the wreck, and it goes out through it and is gone. And from his roof:

The First Signal

...your toy brought guests.

NOW. WHERE WERE WE.

The second quarter

Straight after, he's on his arena's floor again with three-quarters of him left, and the fight goes on to half of him: "SENTINEL's down - and so's what was in it. He's on his arena's floor again, three-quarters of him left. The Watcher has the Omni: when he fires it, hit him." Every half-minute or so the Watcher fires it into him and puts him down on one knee, his armour cracking and burning at the seams, and while he's down everything hurts him half again as much ("The Omni! He's down - hit him now!"). New, too: he puts his blade up at the rip, and bolts of his static come down where each of you is standing (red rings on the floor first: get out of them).

The Forge

Take half of him and it's a cutscene. The Omni puts him on his knee. "...my Faithful." They come down out of their seats to him in flashes of his static, lay their hands on his armour and close its cracks, and chant their glyphs into it: his armour and his blade enchanted, glyphs crawling over them. He gets up and tells you what it was all for.

The First Signal, mended

...is that it?

a gun in the sky. a crew who never asked to come.

they fell for you. remember that.

every signal that ever went out comes home to me.

everything they built up there. every last watt of it -

IS MINE.

He puts his blade up at what's left of SENTINEL and tears every bit of its light out of it, down into him, till it's dark ("he's draining it. all of it.", says the Watcher). (In 1.70.0 it stopped there, on a card: THE LAST BROADCAST, THE FORGE, to be continued. Since 1.71.0 there's no card: the Tearing goes straight on from it.) His kingdom's wall of static stands further out now, past where the wreck came down.

Part four: the Tearing

The Tearing

The Forge goes straight on into the Tearing (in a world where it was forged already, it plays a few seconds after any of you is in his kingdom). THE LAST BROADCAST, THE TEARING, part four. "this sea's too small for me now." He puts his blade straight up, and the sky over his arena tears open, flat across it, as wide as the arena, colour bars round its edge. The Watcher: "...what is he doing?" His sea pours off it, its walls groan, its bridge breaks, and the whole of it is torn up out of his sea ("HOLD ON TO SOMETHING."), and you with it, up into the tear.

The End's guardian

His arena hangs out over the void north of the End's island, a ragged cone of the rock it was torn out of under it, its bridge snapped off short, pieces of it hanging loose in the air round it. "...the End. he's brought it all to the End.", says the Watcher. Up out of the dark comes the End's guardian: the Ender Dragon, taken, its back split open, the parasite all through it and the hive's growths on it. It circles.

The First Signal

the last thing your world kept for itself.

and look what's kept it all this time.

He comes down off his box. The dragon circles his arena; now and then it comes round at one of you and strafes you (its fireballs burst into its breath on the floor), or dives at you, low and fast. It can't be brought down. Only he can do that.

The fight in the End

Half of him is gone already, and this time he fights to a fifth of him (15%). He's forged now, enchanted and alight with SENTINEL's power, and the Watcher still has the Omni and still fires it into him every half-minute or so. Step down onto the floor and he leaps down to you ("AGAIN."). Fall off the edge into the void and his static catches you and puts you back on the floor, and it hurts ("Caught by his static - and put back."). Anyone who comes to his kingdom now is carried on through to his arena in the End ("Carried through to the End - to his arena."), and the End lets you in to it while all this is going on.

The Second Rite

At 15% it's a cutscene. "...not like this." Then "YOU. you'll do." He's up onto the dragon's neck as it comes down at him, his blade in it, riding it round over his arena, and it comes down on the floor. He takes its head off; tears its wings off; reaches into its chest and pulls out its heart. His Faithful come down to him and sing the second rite round him, their glyphs lit in a ring on the floor:

The Faithful, chanting

"head to crown him. wings to bear him."

"heart to keep him. ours, for ever."

Its head over his is his helm: since 1.72.2 its skull forms over his piece by piece, out of nothing, red-hot, its eye sockets lit violet and two pairs of horns swept back between his crown's daggers. Its wings go onto his back; its heart into his chest. "NOW. I AM THE END." His wings open.

On its wings

Since 1.72.0 he takes off on the dragon's wings, and you fight him again in the air over his arena, under a whole new bar: The First Signal, The End (lighter-armoured in the air). He circles over you, then:

  • A dive: reared up, his blade over his head, and down on you head first. The floor bursts where he hits it, and he's down on it for a few seconds, getting up: hit him then.
  • A gust: three great beats of his wings ("DOWN.") and everything in front of him is thrown back across the floor. Mind the edge.
  • Its breath: down low, the dragon's head on him breathes its fire in a sweep across the floor, and it burns there a while.
  • A volley: balls of its fire spat down at you one after another.
  • Seized (once he's lost a third): he swoops down and carries you up in his hand, crushing you ("UP."), and throws you down. Hurt him enough while he's got you and he lets go.
  • His static (once he's lost two-thirds): bolts down out of the sky where you stand.

The Watcher's Omni still knocks him out of the sky now and then, down on the floor and wide open. A bow, a crossbow or a trident helps. Brought down to nothing ("NO -"), he falls out of the air, turning over and over, onto where the rite was, and the Watcher gets up.

The Watcher fights

The Watcher lets the Omni fall and gets up off his knees: "the End was never yours." The First Signal's static throws him back. He catches the blade, and puts his beam, a real beam of shadow out of his hands, into the First Signal's heart: "i've watched you since the first night. i know what you are." Then the Faithful seize the First Signal. A seal opens behind him, black, ringed in their glyphs, and "this isn't over. THIS ISN'T OVER -", and they're gone into it with him, and it's shut.

The Rebirth

Straight after: far out in the dark, the Harbinger's bones. Its skin grows back over them from its skull to its tail, all three of them in it, grown over each other: the Grove's, the Reach's and the hive's. Its eye opens, and a voice that isn't anyone's says: "...again." A door of the hive's, and it goes through; out of another, far off; and into another. THE LAST BROADCAST, THE SECOND RITE, to be continued. The Watcher sends you home: "The Watcher sent you home. THE LAST BROADCAST goes on in its next part (a coming update)."

After it

His arena's gone from his sea, and so is he: only SENTINEL's wreck, and what's left of SENTINEL dark in the rip. "His arena's gone from his sea - and so is he. THE LAST BROADCAST goes on in its next part." The End goes back to sending you home.

Every word he says is on The First Signal speaks, and every word of the Watcher's on The Watcher speaks.

What comes next

"this isn't over." The mod says so too: to be continued. The Faithful have him somewhere even the Watcher can't see, and the Harbinger is going from door to door.
