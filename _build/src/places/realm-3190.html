The second Easter egg, since 1.74.0. Survive Tune-In Town's five nights and a tall man in a trench coat comes knocking. He sells the way into six realms, each one somebody's nightmare with a killer in it, and a house full of puzzles. Put all six right and there's one more door, down into the night it all started.

It waits for the story

Since 1.77.3 the Easter eggs only come once the story's done: the Last Broadcast has signed off. Tune-In Town's flyer, and through it the Peddler and everything on this page, waits till then. Anything already found in a world stays found. To open them in a world now, use /statichollow eggs early (and /statichollow eggs story to go back to waiting).

The Peddler

The evening after the night shift, someone knocks on your door three times. The Peddler is a tall man in a grey trench coat and a wide hat, his face always in its shadow, with a battered suitcase. He has a real voice: he greets you, haggles, warns you and says goodnight. He's taller than a door, so under a low ceiling or through a doorway he stoops, knees bent, looking up from under his hat.

He'd like somewhere to stay: a bed of his own, under a roof, with four walls round it, and not yours. Give him one and he moves in. He opens doors, lets himself into his room, and at night he goes to his bed and sleeps until it's light. Once he's introduced himself he gives everyone he meets his book, Rites & Remedies: finding him a room, the circle and its candles, the rite step by step, the tokens and what to do in each realm, and what you get at the end, with somebody's pencilled warnings in the margins. A spare copy is 1 emerald.

He sells:

  • The old computer, a beige 1999 PC, for four blocks of emerald. A pickaxe takes it up; put it where you like.
  • A ritual circle, tallow candles, and a token for every realm that isn't quiet yet, so the realms can be done in any order.
  • Bottled Daylight (night vision).
  • Three more rites he swears work just as well (below).

When he has something for you (the rewards below), it comes first since 1.77.3: he wakes up, stops whatever he's saying and hands it over, and the chat tells you to go and see him.

The old computer and the 3190 Archive

Use the computer and it dials up, modem screech and all, to a Geocities site from 1999 in a proper old browser (Back, Reload, Home, a location bar, frames, a status bar): a visitor counter that never goes up, a marquee, UNDER CONSTRUCTION and a guestbook. Its pages:

  • SIGHTINGS: photographs people sent in, grainy, flash at night, the date burnt into the corner, and what they saw.
  • ENTITIES and LEGENDS: the doors, and in among them, the rite.
  • MISSING: the faces of the people who went into the realms. Read to the bottom on a computer you didn't buy, and there's a link.
  • TAPES: every tape, and the ones you've found play again from here.
  • E-MAIL: A. Grey's inbox, the families' emails, the people on the tapes, and the last one, to himself.
  • CHAT: a 1999 chat room, live, with the last people in it, and then something else that knows your name.
  • GAMES: WORM. Eat the candles and get longer and faster; after six, something blind gets into the game with you, clicking, coming for the sound. High scores to beat.
  • DEATH TAPES (1.75.5) and the CONTROL PANEL (1.75.5), below.

Leave it alone and its screensaver comes on. The Archive Disk, one of the rewards, shows the webmaster's last post.

The true rite

The LEGENDS page has it. After dark, set out the ritual circle, put candles round it (as many as that realm's door wants), light them with flint and steel, and lay the realm's token in the middle. A door tears open in the air over it, and you have a minute to walk through. The wrong number of candles, or daylight, and they gutter out. When a door opens, some of what lives on the other side comes through first, for you.

The other three rites

  • The Wax Effigy: burn it in the circle and you're pulled into the Wax Man's house, a sixth realm. Its door stays open a while behind you for anyone who wants to follow.
  • The Black Mirror: something looks back out of it and follows you for a day and a night: the Reflection.
  • The Bone Chime: tonight the realms come to you, wave after wave of their things round where you rang it, till dawn.

The six realms

Each is its own dimension with its own sky, fog and sounds, built the first time you go in and furnished with real 3D furniture. Cleanse one and a door home opens where you came in.

  • The Pale Acres: a bleached cornfield higher than your head at a dusk that has days and nights now, with no edge to it, and a farmhouse with bolted doors, a cellar under it and HIS ROOM. Light all five pyres and the corn really burns, row to row, till the field's black stubble. Keep out of the fire; the corn grows back when the realm starts over.
  • The Vacancy: a motel hallway that doesn't end, the lamps buzzing and guttering, guests asleep in the rooms (a noise wakes them), and the Bellhop's own STAFF ONLY room by the lobby.
  • The Mire: black water under a red moon, dead trees hung with moss, a walkway of old planks, her nests round the water and green lamps hung to lure you in.
  • The White Woods: snow, pines, night, and a cabin with a fire. Four of the Archive's missing are frozen into the ice out there, calling for help.
  • The Source: where the Archive is served from. You come in through the webmaster's house in the dark, cables through every room; out its back door, the floor turning to flesh and the four towers. By the door, a chest with Wool Boots (footsteps in wool don't carry), matches, torches and a note.
  • The Wax House: a children's entertainer's house: the party room with the cake still out, his workshop, his room, closets to hide in, grates in every wall, and in the cellar a star poured in wax with five candles burning at its points. Snuff all five and the house lets you go. It's new every time the effigy burns.

The creatures are on Realm 3190's creatures.

The Peddler's rule

Through any realm's door, he's waiting on the other side ("that's cheating"): everything comes off you but food, water, torches and lanterns. He minds it all and gives every bit back the moment that realm is quiet. He leaves you an electric torch: it's dark in there.

Nobody leaves till it's quiet

Die in a realm and it has you back at its door, everything still on you, and every door you'd opened, every puzzle, every pyre lit, bell rung and key turned still done. Logging out or stopping the world is the same. Coming home shuts the way in behind you, and no door takes you twice in three seconds.

The houses

Every realm has a house a long way off from the rest of it, through a door out in the realm: the Mill House (the barn in the Pale Acres), the Staff Wing (the lobby's back door), the Drowned Chapel (the Mire's pump house), the Ranger Lodge (the woodshed by the cabin), the Basement (the Source's garage) and the Wax Works (his shed). Each has thirteen rooms: a safe room where nothing comes in, locked doors (a rusted key, a moon key), dark rooms where you'll need the torch (flame won't keep there), a breaker box, three crests for the gallery's sockets, a four-dial lock, a long dark corridor where the realm's master comes for you (run), and a vault with something the realm needs. Notes on lecterns tell you the codes, and cutscenes play as you go.

  • New answers every time: each house's puzzles and every end room's number are drawn afresh each time it's built, and the notes are written to match. Use a piece to change it; sneak and use it to go back a step.
  • The two-player lock: only where someone else could be there (a server, a LAN world, a second player), the vault has a second lock, two keyholes, and two of you have to turn them within a second and a half.

  • The killer hunts you: a couple of minutes after you're in, and every few minutes after, the realm's master comes into its own house (a door slams somewhere) and walks it room to room, listening for puzzle pieces, guns and running. It can't be killed (a flash bulb or a salt bomb stops it a moment) and it never goes into the safe room. Hide in a wardrobe, unless it saw you get in.
  • The Wax House's cellar hatch: the cellar key opens a hatch in the back room's floor.

Weapons and supplies

Found in the houses: a pistol (its torch goes on it), a revolver, a shotgun, a hunting rifle and a scoped sniper rifle. Hold use to aim, attack to fire, F to reload. Every shot wears a gun; worn out, it jams, and gun parts fix it. Make ammunition from gunpowder and brass casings (scrap metal for the heavier rounds, a precision casing for the sniper's). With TaCZ installed (1.75.3), the houses have TaCZ's Glock 17, AI AWP, M870 and AK-47 instead, with their own rounds and attachments, all made at TaCZ's gun smith table. Things to hit with: a baseball bat, a lead pipe, a combat knife, a machete and a fire axe, mended with duct tape or a whetstone. Green and red herbs (mix them), bandages, first aid spray, batteries, salt bombs, flash bulbs, molotovs and road flares.

Fear

Your nerve builds in the dark (worse in a realm), near the realms' things (worse if one's looking at you or after you) and every time you're hurt. It comes down in good light, in a safe room, by a fire or the Peddler, in the day and asleep. From a third of the way up you hear your heartbeat and the edges of your sight go dark red; past half you hear things nobody else does; past three quarters the dark closes right in now and then. Near the top, for you alone, a realm's door stands open ahead of you, the Host at the edge of your sight, or "CLEANSED", and then it isn't.

What only you see

After dark, now and then and for one player only: the glimpse (with somebody else on the server), one of your friends standing at the edge of the trees, watching you, their name over their head, gone when you look away or walk up; and the steps, footsteps a few paces behind you that stop when you turn. A glimpse comes every half hour to an hour at most, the steps every quarter to half hour. Neither does any harm.

The open doors leak

A realm you've been into and not put right isn't shut all the way (1.75.5). Some nights one or two of its lesser things get out by the circle you went through, loose in the dark till dawn. The Peddler warns you the evening before. Cleanse the realm and it stops.

The First Night

Cleanse all six realms and the Peddler has one more thing for you (1.75.4): the First Candle, the one he lit in his basement on 31 October 1995. Set it on the ritual circle and it lights itself, and a door opens that no rite does. You come in in Alan Grey's basement as it was: his seven candles round the scorch on the floor, his notes on the lectern, the stairs up to a door (open it to go home before it's done), and the north wall broken out into nothing. Boards lead out over it to where the Host is waiting, with a fragment of each of the six realms round it. Fall off the edge and you wake on the basement floor. Put it down and the way home opens, and everyone there finds the seventh tape. Earns The First Night.

The missing tapes

Six camcorder tapes, one for each realm, each somebody who went missing there, and a seventh:

  • Jimmy Mercer, a farm job at the Pale Acres, talking to his brother Dale on the way up the lane.
  • Gary Holt, a sales trip and a motel with a vacancy; his name's already in the guest book.
  • Erik Lindqvist, looking for his sister in the Mire, hearing her answer from under the water.
  • Danny, a dare in the White Woods.
  • Theo, a webcam log: he traced the Archive's server to the Source. Since 1.77.3 his tape shows his computer: the Archive and his DOS box, then his own webcam with him in it, then his name typing itself.
  • Kyle, episode four of his ghost-hunting show: the Candle Man's house.
  • Alan Grey, his own, 31 October 1995, from the First Night.

A realm's tape turns up in your inventory when you cleanse it (or go back into one already cleansed); Kyle's is in the barrel on the Wax House porch. Use one on the old computer and you're taken into it: a 3D cutscene of the place it was filmed, the people on it and what came for them, all through the camcorder (its picture, timecode, tracking and what was said), a minute and a half to two minutes each. Nothing can touch you while it plays; Esc stops it.

Keepsakes and death tapes

Keepsakes (1.75.5): two of the missing people's things are hidden in each realm's house, in different chests each time it's built: Jimmy's cap, Dale's letter, Gary's order book, Rosa's locket, Theo's glasses, Kyle's press pass and the rest. The journal keeps which you've found.

Death tapes (1.75.5): die in a realm, or the First Night, and a tape of your last half minute turns up on the Archive's DEATH TAPES page. Play it and you're back there, seen through your own eyes. Sneak to stop it. The newest twelve are kept, in the world's folder.

The journal, the walkies and the glass

  • The Field Journal (a book, any coal and a string): the doors, every creature you've met, the tapes you've found, and since 1.75.5 the Bestiary: a pencil sketch of everything you've seen, how to live through it once you have, and the last of it once its realm is quiet. Since 1.75.9, whatever you've filmed is marked on tape.
  • Walkie-Talkies (1.75.5; an iron nugget over iron-redstone-iron over iron-copper-iron makes two): use one and everyone else with one hears who, how far and which way. With Simple Voice Chat, hold it and talk to all of them at any distance. It crackles when a realm creature is near you, and the things near the one talking hear it. Since 1.76.0 they reach other dimensions too, and a friend in trouble cuts in on everyone's radio.
  • The Looking Glass, Mirror Shards, the Shard Ward and the Mirror Snare: see the Reflection.

The rewards

When five are clean, talk to the Peddler. He has three things for you:

  • The 3190 Key: a weapon. What it strikes locks up; right-click and you're at wherever you're looking.
  • The Hound: a big gaunt black dog with pale eyes and an old collar with a rusted 3190 tag, tame and yours. His bay is a howl that knocks back what it faces and takes the strength out of it. He learned a lot more later: see the Hound.
  • The Archive Disk: put it in the old computer for the webmaster's last post.

All six put right, he gives you the First Candle (above).

Voices (Simple Voice Chat)

With Simple Voice Chat on the server and in your game, the realms' things hear you talk: how far depends on how loud you are, from a whisper (a few blocks) to shouting (thirty). Some of them use voices they've heard in proximity chat, only ever heard by the player they're after: the Reflection's echo and call, the voice through the Bellhop's door ("Let me in") and the call from the Lantern Man's light. They only use the last few things each player said out loud in proximity chat, never in a group, kept in memory only and gone when that player leaves. /myvoice off stops anything copying your voice; an operator can turn it off for everyone with /statichollow r3190 voices off. It has its own volume slider in the voice chat's settings. Without Simple Voice Chat, none of this happens.
