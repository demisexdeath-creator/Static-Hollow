Since 1.55.0 none of Minecraft's own music plays in Static Hollow. Wherever and whenever the game would have played one of its pieces, one of the mod's plays instead: in the overworld, in Creative, under water, in the Nether, in the End, during the dragon's fight and over the credits. And it follows the story.

The soundtrack's own track list has 63 pieces, about 2 hours 20 minutes of music, as of 1.59.0. Chapters eight to ten, Tune-In Town and the new country have added more since: see music since the track list.

How it plays

  • Minecraft's settings still count. Its Music volume and Music Frequency decide how loud and how often, as they always did.
  • Now Playing. Minecraft's "Now Playing" corner names each piece: "Static Hollow - The Deep", or on the title screen "Static Hollow - Lullaby".
  • The place or the chapter. Each time a new piece is due it's either the lullaby of the place you're in or one of the story chapter's own pieces. The further the story has got, the more often it's the chapter's (see the table below).
  • Nothing over the scores. While one of the mod's own scores is playing, a boss fight's, a place's (Rosemont's) or a cutscene's, the background music stops and nothing new starts over it until it's done.
  • Under the Dead Water each water has its own piece, and that comes first whenever you're under.

Music since the track list

Everything below came after the soundtrack's track list (1.59.0), so none of it has a track number yet.

Chapter eight: the Dead Water

  • The Rig: the cutscene as you come down onto the rig.
  • Dead Water: the story's own piece for chapter eight (with The First Signal and Low Tide).
  • One piece for each of the Dead Water's waters, played while you're under: the Grey Shelf, the Haemal Reach, the Wireweed, the Lantern Fields, the Ossuary Flats, the Brine Sink, the Molar Reef, the Drowned Mycelia, the Rime Shelf, the Ember Vents and the Carrier Wave. In the twelfth, the Black Below, there's no music: only the sound of the ocean.

See the Dead Water for the waters themselves.

Chapter nine: the Harbinger

  • The Turning: its own score, for the cutscene where the Undertow eats both hearts and comes back mad.
  • The Harbinger: for the cutscene three days later, when the Harbinger comes out of the husk.

See chapter nine.

Chapter ten: Stay Tuned

  • On Air: the Broadcast's score in his kingdom. It comes back for the Coronation, the Forge and the arrival in the End.
  • Get Ready: the Broadcast's last part, at your house.
  • Stay Tuned: the story's own piece for chapter ten, once the Broadcast has aired.
  • The Last Broadcast: the fight with him in his arena and over it in the End, on a loop for as long as the fight goes on.
  • Off the Air: written for the end of chapter ten at his war table, which the mod still holds back. Not heard yet in 1.72.2.

The rest of the Last Broadcast's cutscenes borrow older scores. The Fall and the Tearing play The Omni Ray, the Second Rite plays All of Them (the Chorus, whole's fight), and the Rebirth plays It Runs. The wall, the Refusal and the Mast have no music at all. See chapter ten and The Last Broadcast.

Tune-In Town

  • The Showtape: the show's own song, on the music disc you're paid with for the sixth night. It runs 1:36, till the tape runs slow and something else is on it.
  • The band, live: as the place's owner you can have the band play the show's song on the show stage after hours (since 1.65.5).
  • The music box in the Prize Corner: keep it wound on the night shift, or Jack Jangle is out.

See Tune-In Town.

The Easter eggs

  • The Starlight Revue (1.75.8): five records of the revue's own music, below. The Roller Rink's jukebox plays them by itself, and in the Encore the cast perform to the Overture.
  • Lullabelle's music box: on the Annex's night shift it means she's out dancing through the booth (hold your breath). In the Moon Room she sings, and her song puts you to sleep on your feet. Pearl sings too, out of the water.
  • The calliope at Crane's Midnight Fair (1.75.9): round and round at the carousel, and the first you hear of the fair's gate on a full-moon night.
  • The ice cream truck's tune (1.77.0): Dusk Delights plays it as it comes. Some nights it slows down and goes off-key, and that's when it's hunting.

See the Starlight Annex, Crane's Midnight Fair and Dusk Delights.
