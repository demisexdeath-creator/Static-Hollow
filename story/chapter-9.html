    Chapter nine

    The Harbinger

    three days

    On this page
    1. The Turning
    2. Three days
    3. The Harbinger
    4. The Remote (Mutated)
    5. What comes next

    Orpheus has all three of her modules, and the sea goes wrong. The Undertow comes up out of the water by the rig and roars, goes down through the Grove and through the Drowned Heart, and comes back as something its maker never made.

    Chapter Nine arrived in 1.63.23, and 1.63.24 and 1.63.25 mended it. The title card reads CHAPTER NINE, THE HARBINGER, three days. It happens in the Dead Water, round the rig, and it has two pieces of music of its own, The Turning and The Harbinger. What it makes is the Harbinger.

    The Turning

    Once Orpheus has all three of her modules in her bay (the pressure plating, the depth module and the UV field module), it plays, for everyone in the Dead Water: off whatever you're in (she's put back in her clamps), out of any screen, up onto the rig's helipad. A full cutscene, with its own score.

    The Undertow comes up out of the sea by the rig and roars. Then it goes. To the Drowned Mycelia, down through the roof of the Grove's hollow, and it eats the Grove Heart, which has one thing left to say, in a voice that isn't anyone's: "...no. not you. not you." To the Haemal Reach, and through the Drowned Heart. With both in it, it turns: the Grove's fungus up its left side, pale, gilled, caps growing out of it; the Reach's flesh up its right, raw and veined, its light gone red. It comes back mad, up out of the sea hundreds of blocks and down, again and again round the rig, and the last time it comes down by the rig, and lies there.

    Down under where he went in, the sleeping Watcher wakes. His bubble breaks, and he comes up out of the sea and onto the helipad beside you. After his first words, you point at it.

    That's CHAPTER NINE, and The Turning ("Watch the Undertow eat both hearts - and come down by the rig"). The chat says: "Chapter Nine: The Harbinger. It lies in the sea by the rig, both infections all over it - he says it's sleeping. Three days."

    Three days

    It lies in the sea north-east of the rig, its jaws about a hundred blocks off (on your left, looking out to sea from the helipad): the husk, its eyes shut, both infections all over it, spores steaming off one side and blood off the other. From the rig you can hear its heart, beating faster as the days go. There's no fight. It just lies there. And with it lying there, nothing comes up out of the Black Below, the Grove's Heart doesn't grow back, and the Drowned Heart doesn't beat.

    The Watcher keeps watch at the helipad's rail, and has something to say each day when you come near him:

    The first day

    ???i've been through everything i ever made. it isn't in any of it. where did it come from?

    The second day

    ???the lab's log said three days. that's how long the enzyme takes to finish what it starts.

    The third day

    ???tomorrow. whatever it's turning into, it's nearly done. stay near.

    The three days are 72,000 ticks. The Dead Water's sky never moves and nobody sleeps there, so they count by the Overworld's clock (since 1.63.24): nights slept through at home, or /time add, bring the Harbinger on.

    The Harbinger

    Three days on, the next time you're in the Dead Water, it happens. Its bones come out through its mouth: a skull, a spine and its ribs, strung all through with the Grove's strings out of the heart in its ribs, where the Grove's heart and the Reach's are going into one ("Two hearts become one"). It rises over the sea, hung on its strings, and brings up the static heart at your feet, one for each of you. Then the heart speaks to you both, in two voices whispering as one:

    The Grove's voice and the Reach's, together

    thank you for making us one.

    this gift will not be wasted.

    we would want you to join us. to be stronger. both of you.

    but we have more important work to do.

    It tears a way out of the Dead Water, half the Grove's and half the Reach's, and goes through it. The husk goes down into the sea.

    That's This Gift Will Not Be Wasted ("See what came out of it after three days"). The chat says: "It's gone through. The static heart it brought up is on the helipad - put it in the Remote (craft them together)."

    The Remote (Mutated)

    What it brought up is the Static Heart (Mutated), "Thrown up by the Harbinger": "The Undertow's static, grown through with the Grove's threads and the Reach's flesh. It still beats - a little faster than yours." Craft it with the Broken Remote (or the mended one) and you have the Remote (Mutated): "It works again - and it roars".

    • Press it: the Hub (and in the Hub, home). Crouch and press it: the Dead Water.
    • Hold it, then let go: the Undertow's roar comes out of your hand. Everything near you that isn't with you is thrown back, torn at and slowed (half as far again under water), and its echo makes everything alive for 48 blocks round glow. Then it's spent for fifteen seconds. The first time is Its Roar, In Your Hand.

    So you're not stranded any more, whether or not you ever mended the Remote with a Signal Organ. See Every item and block.

    What comes next

    "i'll meet you at the hub." The Harbinger has gone through its way out, and nobody has said where it goes. The next time you're home, back at your bed or your spawn in the Overworld, or home by the Remote, something cuts in on you: Chapter Ten: Stay Tuned.
